### Topic 1.1 — What Is a Business?

**Q1** | BU02 Jan 2024 Q2 adapted | 1 mark | MCQ

> A business's financial data shows:
>
> | | Year 1 | Year 2 |
> |---|---|---|
> | Sales revenue | $480,000 | $520,000 |
> | Cost of goods sold | $300,000 | $340,000 |
>
> Which statement is **best supported** by the data?
>
> (A) Gross profit decreased from Year 1 to Year 2
> (B) The gross profit margin increased from Year 1 to Year 2
> (C) The business became less profitable in Year 2
> (D) Revenue and costs increased by the same amount

**AP Topic:** 1.1 What Is a Business · **Knowledge point:** Profit = Revenue − Total Cost; gross profit margin interpretation
**Skill:** 1.B Interpret data

---

**Q2** | BU01 Jan 2024 Q6 adapted | 3 marks | Short explain

> A sole trader is considering converting to a public limited company (PLC) to raise capital for growth.
>
> Explain **one benefit** to the business of selling shares to the public.

**AP Topic:** 1.1 What Is a Business · **Knowledge point:** Business types (sole trader vs. PLC); sources of financial capital
**Skill:** 1.A Describe

---

**Q3** | BU01 Jan 2023 Q13 adapted | 12 marks | Evaluate

> **CafeLocal** is a successful private limited company that owns three coffee shops. Revenue has grown 30% per year for three years. The founder wants to open ten more locations but lacks the capital.
>
> CafeLocal is considering changing to a public limited company (PLC) to raise funds by selling shares.
>
> Assess the arguments **for and against** CafeLocal changing to a PLC. Make a judgement.

**AP Topic:** 1.1 What Is a Business · **Knowledge point:** Business ownership types; trade-offs of going public (capital access vs. loss of control)
**Skill:** 3.A Establish criteria, 3.D Recommend

### Topic 1.2 — Markets and Competitive Advantage

**Q4** | BU01 Jan 2024 Q4 adapted | 2 marks | Calculation

> In 2022, the total market size for electric scooters was $12,000,000. The market grew by 8% between 2022 and 2023. Sales of VoltRide scooters in 2023 were $950,000.
>
> Calculate VoltRide's market share in 2023. Give your answer to one decimal place.

**AP Topic:** 1.2 Markets and Competitive Advantage · **Knowledge point:** Market share = (firm sales ÷ total market size) × 100
**Skill:** 1.B Calculate

### Topic 1.4 — How Business Ideas Originate

**Q11** | AQA-style adapted | 3 marks | Short explain

**AP Topic:** 1.4 How Business Ideas Originate · **Knowledge point:** Gap (unmet need); viability (willing and able buyers)

> Min plc is considering a **takeover** of **Sung Healthcare Ltd**, a Singapore-based company that has designed innovative surgical robots.
