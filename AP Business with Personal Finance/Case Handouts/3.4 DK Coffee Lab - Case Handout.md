# 3.4 DK Coffee Lab — Business Expenses

> **Topic 3.4 Business Expenses** · Read before class. Be ready to discuss.

---

## Background

**DK Coffee Lab** is a specialty coffee roaster and cafe founded in 2022 in Portland, Oregon, by **Daniel Kim**, a former software engineer. The company operates two business lines: **retail cafes** (selling brewed drinks and pastries directly to consumers) and a **wholesale roasting operation** (selling freshly roasted beans to local restaurants, offices, and grocery stores). As of 2025, DK Coffee Lab operates **three cafe locations** in Portland and a dedicated **roasting facility** that supplies beans to over 40 wholesale accounts.

Daniel started DK Coffee Lab with **$120,000** in personal savings and a small loan from a family member. He did not take outside investment. The first cafe opened in a 900-square-foot storefront in Portland's Alberta Arts District after five months of planning, legal setup, and build-out.

DK Coffee Lab's competitive advantage is **direct-trade sourcing**: Daniel travels to coffee farms in Colombia, Ethiopia, and Guatemala twice a year to personally select beans, negotiating directly with farmers rather than going through commodity brokers. This sourcing model produces higher-quality coffee but creates **higher and more variable direct costs** than competitors who buy commodity-grade beans on the open market.

As of 2025, DK Coffee Lab generates approximately **$1.8 million in annual revenue** across its three cafes and wholesale accounts. The company employs **28 people**. The business is **profitable but thin-margin**: net income is approximately 6–8% of revenue, which is typical for the specialty coffee industry.
