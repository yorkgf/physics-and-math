# 2.6 观夏 To Summer — Case Handout

**Topic:** 2.6 Place and Channels

---

## Background

观夏 (To Summer) is a Chinese niche fragrance brand founded in 2019 in Beijing by **Elvis Liu** (刘慧璞). The brand specializes in Chinese cultural fragrances — incense, candles, perfumes, and home scent products — all inspired by traditional Chinese aesthetics and natural ingredients.

The brand's Chinese name "观夏" means "observing summer," evoking a sense of contemplation and seasonal beauty. Its English name "To Summer" carries the same poetic feeling. The brand positions itself around **"东方人文香" (Eastern humanistic fragrance)** — fragrances that tell stories about Chinese landscapes, seasons, poetry, and cultural memory.

From its founding, 观夏 took a radically different approach to distribution: it **refused to sell on any third-party platform** (no Tmall, no JD.com, no Douyin Shop) and limited sales to its own **WeChat mini-program** (with scheduled drops) and a small number of **boutique experience stores**. This deliberate channel restriction became the brand's defining characteristic — and its competitive advantage.

As of 2024, 观夏 operates fewer than 10 physical stores across China, yet commands some of the highest prices in the Chinese fragrance market and has a devoted following among young, educated Chinese consumers.

## Key Events

| Year | Event |
|------|-------|
| 2019 | **Elvis Liu** founds 观夏 in Beijing; launches with a small collection of home fragrance products |
| 2019 | Opens first boutique in **Beijing's Guozijian (国子监)** hutong — a restored traditional courtyard that blends fragrance retail with cultural experience |
| 2019 | Launches **WeChat mini-program** as the ONLY online sales channel — products available only during scheduled weekly "drops" |
| 2020 | Refuses offers from **Tmall, JD.com, and Douyin** to join their platforms — maintains direct-channel-only strategy |
| 2020 | Opens **Shanghai Hunan Road (湖南路)** boutique — a restored old Shanghai lane house with art installations and a fragrance library |
| 2021 | Products regularly **sell out within minutes** during WeChat drops; customers share screenshots on Xiaohongshu |
| 2021 | Launches signature fragrance collection inspired by Chinese landscapes: "昆仑煮雪" (Kunlun Snow), "颐和金桂" (Summer Palace Osmanthus) |
| 2022 | Opens **Chengdu Citang Street (祠堂街)** boutique — restored historical building; becomes a cultural landmark |
| 2022 | Media coverage of 观夏's stores as **cultural spaces** — generates significant PR without paid advertising |
| 2023 | Expands to additional cities including **Shenzhen, Hangzhou, Nanjing** — each store uniquely designed to reflect local culture |
| 2024 | Continues to operate **fewer than 10 stores** — deliberately slow expansion to maintain exclusivity |
| 2024 | Maintains **zero presence** on any third-party e-commerce platform; WeChat mini-program remains the only online channel |

## Questions

**1. Channel Type Classification**
观夏 sells only through its WeChat mini-program and its own boutique stores. What type of channel are each of these? Why does 观夏 refuse to use indirect channels like Tmall or Sephora?

**2. Channel Selection Trade-offs**
观夏 deliberately limits its reach by refusing to sell on major platforms. What does 观夏 gain from this strategy, and what does it sacrifice? Is this a good trade-off?

**3. The WeChat Mini-Program as Channel Innovation**
观夏's WeChat mini-program only sells products during scheduled "drops" — most products sell out in minutes. How does this differ from a normal e-commerce store (like Tmall), and why does this serve 观夏's channel strategy?

**4. Store as Brand Experience**
观夏's boutique stores are described as "brand temples" rather than retail locations. How does this approach to physical stores differ from a traditional retailer like Sephora? What does 观夏 gain by investing so much in the store experience?

**5. Channel Comparison**
Stanley expanded its channels from hardware stores to Target + DTC. 观夏 refuses all third-party channels and sells only directly. Both strategies are successful. Why can two opposite strategies both work?

**6. Growth Decision**
观夏 is currently considering whether to open stores in 5 more cities or to finally launch on Tmall. Which option would you recommend, and why?
