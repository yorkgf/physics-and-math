## ANSWERS (teacher · students stop here)

### A (6)
| # | Line | Category | Sits |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Cost of sales | **COGS** | above gross profit |
| 2 | Demand creation expense | **Operating expense** (selling) | below gross profit |
| 3 | Operating overhead expense | **Operating expense** (G&A) | below gross profit |
| 4 | Total S&A | **Operating expense** (the two above, summed) | below gross profit |
| 5 | Interest (income) expense, net | **Interest** | below operating profit; net income adds back |
| 6 | Other (income) expense, net | **Out of scope** (non-operating, non-interest) | above pretax income; note it, do not analyse it |

**Operating profit = gross profit − total S&A** = 19,911 − 16,114 = **3,797** (FY2026); 19,790 − 16,088 = **3,702** (FY2025).

### B1 (3)
Gross profit **19,911** ✓　S&A **16,114** ✓　OP FY26 **3,797**　OP FY25 **3,702**

### B2 (4)
| Margin | FY2026 | FY2025 |
|---|---|---|
| Gross | **42.9%** | 42.7% |
| Operating | **8.2%** | 8.0% |
| Net | **6.7%** | 7.0% |
| Effective tax rate | **20.3%** | 17.1% |

### B3 (3)
Revenue **+0.19%**　Net income **−3.45%**

### C (5)
| Line | FY26 | FY25 | Change | Favourable? |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Gross profit | 19,911 | 19,790 | **+121** | yes |
| Total S&A | 16,114 | 16,088 | **+26** | no |
| Interest (income) expense, net | (50) | (107) | **+57** (less income) | no |
| Other (income) expense, net | (53) | (76) | **+23** (less income) | no |
| Income tax | 792 | 666 | **+126** | no |
| Net income | 3,108 | 3,219 | **−111** | — |

**Answer: the tax line.** Operating performance actually improved slightly (operating profit +95, pretax +15); the entire fall comes from tax +126 as the effective rate moved 17.1% → 20.3%.
**Improved margin:** gross margin, +0.2 pt (42.7% → 42.9%).

### D (5)
| Line | FY2027 |
|---|---|
| Revenue | 46,398 × 1.03 = **47,790** |
| Gross profit | 47,790 × 42.9% = **20,502** |
| Total S&A | 16,114 × 1.03 = **16,597** |
| Pretax income | **3,905** |
| Tax | 3,905 × 20.3% = **793** |
| **Net income** | **3,112** |

Assumption: **both COGS and S&A scale with revenue** — i.e. planned expenses and supply-chain inputs move with volume (3.6.D.4). A student who assumes fixed costs should be marked down; the projection must state which cost is variable.

### E (4)
1. **Demand creation placed inside COGS** → gross profit should be **19,911** (marketing is an operating expense).
2. **Operating profit set equal to gross profit** → must subtract total S&A 16,114 = **3,797**.
3. **Tax is not 25%**: the reported tax is **792** (effective 20.3%); if forecasting, apply **20.3% to a correct pretax figure**, not 25% to 3,900.
→ Correct net income = **3,108**.

### F (5)
1. All of it went to **S&A**: 16,114 = **34.7% of revenue** (demand creation 10.2%, overhead 24.5%); 42.9% − 34.7% ≈ 8%.
2. The **trend**: revenue 51,362 (FY2024) → 46,309 → 46,398. One year alone hides that revenue is still below FY2024.
3. **Planned expenses** — ask for the marketing budget, store openings and product-launch plan; demand creation is a planned spend, not a production function.

### Common errors
- Putting demand creation in COGS (moves the gross margin)
- Reporting operating profit without subtracting total S&A
- Applying a textbook 25% tax rate to a real statement (real companies have pre-tax adjustments)
- Treating `Other (income) expense, net` as an operating expense
