# Delta 10-K — one input worth 19–24% of operating expense (3.6.D.4)

**Verbatim:**

> Our results of operations are significantly impacted by changes in the price of aircraft fuel. Fuel costs represented 19%, 21% and 24% of our operating expense in 2024, 2023 and 2022, respectively. Fuel prices are highly volatile and at times have adjusted substantially in relatively short periods of time. Between 2022 and 2024, our average fuel price per gallon has ranged from a monthly high of $4.25 in June 2022 to a monthly low of $2.29 in November 2024.

**Source:** https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/27904/000002790425000004/dal-20241231.htm (search: `average fuel price per gallon`)
**Also in this folder as a one-page PDF:** `Delta 10-K FY2024 - Fuel share of opex.pdf`

## What the passage does

| Number | What it teaches |
|---|---|
| Fuel = **19% / 21% / 24%** of operating expense (2024 / 2023 / 2022) | Operating expense is not arbitrary: a **single input price** explains a fifth of it |
| Average price per gallon ranged **$2.29 – $4.25** | A near-twofold swing — which is why "the fuel assumption" is the first cell in next year's budget |

**One line:** an airline's budget does not start with "how many tickets"; it starts with **dollars per gallon** — the clearest illustration of D.4's "operating expenses are estimated from the supply chain".
