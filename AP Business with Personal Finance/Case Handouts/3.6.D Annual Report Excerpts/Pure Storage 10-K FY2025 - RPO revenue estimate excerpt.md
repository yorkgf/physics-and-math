# Pure Storage 10-K — estimating revenue from contract backlog (3.6.D.3)

**Verbatim:**

> We expect to recognize approximately 48% of total RPO over the next 12 months, and the remainder thereafter. RPO is expected to increase as our subscription services business grows over time.

**Source:** https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1474432/000162828025015044/pstg-20250202.htm (search: `We expect to recognize approximately`)
**Also in this folder as a one-page PDF:** `Pure Storage 10-K FY2025 - RPO revenue estimate.pdf`

## What the passage does

| 3.6.D.3 source | What the filing actually does |
|---|---|
| **Trends** | "RPO is expected to increase **as our subscription services business grows**" — revenue is projected from the growth trend of the subscription base |
| Contract backlog | RPO (remaining performance obligations) split **48% in the next 12 months / 52% later** |

**One line:** subscription businesses do not estimate next year's revenue from a sales target; they estimate it from **contracts already signed** multiplied by the pace at which those contracts convert.
