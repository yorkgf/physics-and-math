# Delta 10-K — 一个投入品占运营费用 19–24%（3.6.D.4）

**原文（verbatim）：**

> Our results of operations are significantly impacted by changes in the price of aircraft fuel. Fuel costs represented 19%, 21% and 24% of our operating expense in 2024, 2023 and 2022, respectively. Fuel prices are highly volatile and at times have adjusted substantially in relatively short periods of time. Between 2022 and 2024, our average fuel price per gallon has ranged from a monthly high of $4.25 in June 2022 to a monthly low of $2.29 in November 2024.

**出处：** https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/27904/000002790425000004/dal-20241231.htm （检索词：`average fuel price per gallon`）

## 这一段在做什么

| 数字 | 教学用途 |
|---|---|
| 燃油占 **operating expense 的 19% / 21% / 24%**（2024/2023/2022） | 说明 opex 不是“拍出来的”，可以被**单一投入品价格**解释 |
| 每加仑均价 **$2.29 – $4.25** | 波动幅度接近 2 倍 → 预算里那个“油价假设”有多关键 |

**一句话：** 航空公司做下一年预算，第一个假设不是“卖多少票”，而是**每加仑油价**——这正是 D.4 说的 “operating expenses are estimated from the supply chain”。

## 课堂问法

1. 若油价从 $2.29 涨到 $4.25，而票价不变，operating profit 会怎样？（可让学生用 3.6 的阶梯推）
2. 航空公司为什么用“每加仑”而不是“总油费”来报告？这和 COGS 的“单位成本”思路有什么共通处？
3. 哪一类企业和你所在的 Business Canvas 项目最像——靠单一投入品，还是靠多项投入品？
