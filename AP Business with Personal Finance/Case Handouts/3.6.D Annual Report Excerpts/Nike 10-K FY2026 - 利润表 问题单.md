## 📝 ANSWERS (teacher · students stop here)

### A. Structure (6)

| # | Line | Category | Sits |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Cost of sales | **COGS** | above gross profit |
| 2 | Demand creation expense | **Operating expense** (selling) | below gross profit |
| 3 | Operating overhead expense | **Operating expense** (general & administrative) | below gross profit |
| 4 | Total S&A | **Operating expense** (the two above, summed) | below gross profit |
| 5 | Interest (income) expense, net | **Interest** | below operating profit; a net *income* adds back |
| 6 | Other (income) expense, net | **Out of scope** (non-operating, non-interest) | sits above pretax income; note it, do not analyse it |

**"Operating profit" = gross profit − total S&A** = 19,911 − 16,114 = **3,797** (FY2026); 19,790 − 16,088 = **3,702** (FY2025).

### B1 (3)
Gross profit 46,398 − 26,487 = **19,911** ✓　S&A 4,754 + 11,360 = **16,114** ✓　OP FY26 **3,797**　OP FY25 **3,702**

### B2 (4)
| Margin | FY2026 | FY2025 |
|---|---|---|
| Gross | **42.9%** | 42.7% |
| Operating | **8.2%** | 8.0% |
| Net | **6.7%** | 7.0% |
| Effective tax rate | **20.3%** (792/3,900) | 17.1% (666/3,885) |

### B3 (3)
Revenue **+0.19%**　Net income **−3.45%**

### C. Diagnosis (5)
| Line | FY26 | FY25 | Change | Favourable? |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Gross profit | 19,911 | 19,790 | **+121** | ✓ |
| Total S&A | 16,114 | 16,088 | **+26** | ✗ |
| Interest (income) expense, net | (50) | (107) | **+57** (less income) | ✗ |
| Other (income) expense, net | (53) | (76) | **+23** (less income) | ✗ |
| Income tax | 792 | 666 | **+126** | ✗ |
| Net income | 3,108 | 3,219 | **−111** | — |

**答案：税。** 运营层面其实**略有改善**（operating profit +95，pretax +15）；净利下滑 111 全部来自税额 +126（有效税率 17.1% → 20.3%）。
**改善的 margin：** gross margin，+0.2 个百分点（42.7% → 42.9%）。

### D. Projection (5)
| Line | FY2027 |
|---|---|
| Revenue | 46,398 × 1.03 = **47,790** |
| Gross profit | 47,790 × 42.9% = **20,502** |
| Total S&A | 16,114 × 1.03 = **16,597** |
| Pretax income | **3,905** |
| Tax | 3,905 × 20.3% = **793** |
| **Net income** | **3,112** |

Assumption: **both COGS and S&A are treated as scaling with revenue**（即成本按销售规模同比例增长）——这正是 3.6.D.4 的 "planned expenses + supply chain"：COGS 随产量走，S&A 按计划（营销/人工）走。若学生答"成本固定"则 pretax 会更高，应扣分。

### E. Errors (4)
1. **Demand creation 被算进 COGS** → gross profit 应为 **19,911**（营销是 opex，不进 COGS）。
2. **Operating profit 只写了 gross profit** → 必须再减 total S&A 16,114 = **3,797**。
3. **税不是 25%**：报表现成的税是 **792**（有效税率 20.3%）；若要做预测，应把 **20.3% 乘在 pretax 上**，而不是用一个不含其它项目的 3,900 再套 25%。
→ 正确 net income = **3,108**。

### F. Short answers (5)
1. 全部去了 **S&A**：total S&A = 16,114，占收入 **34.7%**（其中 demand creation 10.2%、operating overhead 24.5%），42.9% − 34.7% ≈ 8%。（2）
2. 看到**趋势**：FY2024 收入 51,362 → FY2025 46,309 → FY2026 46,398；单一年份看不出收入已连续两年低于 FY2024。（1）
3. 用 **planned expenses（营销计划）**：问公司要 marketing 预算与计划开店数/新品投放节奏；demand creation 是"计划性支出"，不是产量函数。（2）

### 常见错误
- 把 demand creation 当 COGS（→ gross margin 虚高或虚低）
- 忘减 total S&A 就报 operating profit
- 用 25% 这个"课本税率"套真实报表（真实公司有 pre-tax adjustments）
- 把 `Other (income) expense, net` 当成 operating expense
