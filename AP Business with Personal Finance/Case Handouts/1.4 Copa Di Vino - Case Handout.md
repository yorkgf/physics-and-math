# Copa Di Vino — Case Handout

> **Topic 1.4 How Business Ideas Originate** · Read before class. Be ready to discuss.

---

## Background

Copa Di Vino is an American company that produces single-serve, ready-to-drink wine in patented sealed cups — wine by the glass, no bottle required. The company was founded in 2005 by James Martin in The Dalles, Oregon.

Martin discovered the concept of wine in a sealed cup while traveling in southern France. He brought the packaging technology back to the U.S. and built a brand around it. The company invested in patented packaging technology, meaning competitors could not legally copy the container design. Copa Di Vino's products are sold in convenience stores, stadiums, airlines, and grocery stores.

Copa Di Vino is a well-known case in entrepreneurship education because James Martin appeared on Shark Tank twice — in Season 2 (2011) and Season 7 (2016) — and turned down offers both times. In Season 2, the sharks offered $600,000 for 51% equity; Martin rejected the deal, choosing to grow the company on his own terms. Despite rejecting the deals, the Shark Tank appearances generated significant brand awareness, and sales grew substantially after each appearance.

## Key Events

| Year | Event |
|---|---|
| **2005** | James Martin founds Copa Di Vino after discovering sealed-cup wine packaging in France |
| **2005–2010** | Slow early growth; builds distribution in Pacific Northwest; invests in patented packaging technology |
| **2011** | Appears on Shark Tank Season 2, Episode 5; sharks offer $600,000 for 51% equity; Martin rejects the deal |
| **2011–2016** | Shark Tank exposure drives brand awareness; sales grow significantly; distribution expands nationally |
| **2016** | Appears on Shark Tank Season 7 (follow-up episode); receives another offer; Martin rejects again |
| **2016–present** | Continues independent growth; product line expands; distribution in major retail chains, stadiums, and airlines |

(A) Identify the source of the business idea that became Copa Di Vino.

(B) Explain how Martin turned this observation into a business opportunity in the United States.
