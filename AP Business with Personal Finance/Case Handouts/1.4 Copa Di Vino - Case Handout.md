# Copa Di Vino — Case Handout

> **Topic 1.4 How Business Ideas Originate** · Read before class. Be ready to discuss.

---

## 1. Company Introduction

**Copa Di Vino** is an American company that produces **single-serve, ready-to-drink wine** in patented sealed cups — wine by the glass, no bottle required.

- **Founded:** 2005 by **James Martin** in The Dalles, Oregon
- **Product:** Premium wine in a patented single-serve container (187 ml), sold in convenience stores, stadiums, airlines, and grocery stores
- **Founder background:** Martin discovered the concept of wine in a sealed cup while traveling in southern France. He brought the packaging technology back to the U.S. and built a brand around it.
- **Industry:** Beverage / Alcoholic drinks

Copa Di Vino is notable in entrepreneurship education because James Martin **appeared on Shark Tank twice** — and **turned down offers both times**, choosing to grow the company on his own terms.

---

## 2. Key Events

| Year | Event |
|---|---|
| **2005** | James Martin founds Copa Di Vino after discovering sealed-cup wine packaging in France |
| **2005–2010** | Slow early growth; builds distribution in Pacific Northwest; invests in patented packaging technology |
| **2011** | Appears on **Shark Tank Season 2, Episode 5** — the sharks offer $600,000 for 51% equity; Martin **rejects the deal** |
| **2011–2016** | Shark Tank exposure drives massive brand awareness; sales grow significantly; distribution expands nationally |
| **2016** | Appears on **Shark Tank Season 7** (follow-up episode) — receives another offer; Martin **rejects again** |
| **2016–present** | Continues independent growth; product line expands; distribution in major retail chains, stadiums, and airlines |
