# 2.4 Genki Forest (元气森林) — Case Handout

> **Topic 2.4 Product** · Read before class. Be ready to discuss.

---

## Background

Genki Forest (元气森林) is a Chinese beverage company founded in 2016 by **Binsen Tang** (唐彬森), a serial tech entrepreneur. The company produces sugar-free, zero-calorie sparkling water and other health-focused beverages. By 2021, annual sales reached approximately RMB 7 billion (~$1 billion), and the company was valued at $6 billion after its most recent funding round. Genki Forest products are now sold in over 40 countries.

Tang previously founded **ELEX Technology**, China's third-largest gaming company, which he sold for **$434 million** in 2014. He then established **Challenger Ventures**, an investment firm supporting Chinese FMCG entrepreneurs. Tang and his founding team had **zero experience in the beverage industry** — they were all tech entrepreneurs.

Genki Forest uses the Japanese kanji character **'気' (ki)** instead of the simplified Chinese '气' on its packaging, and the brand's visual identity is designed to appear Japanese. The company positions itself as a **"technology company"** rather than a traditional beverage company, applying data analytics, user research, and rapid product iteration — practices borrowed from the tech industry — to develop and market its drinks.

## Key Events

| Year | Event |
|------|-------|
| 2014 | Binsen Tang sells ELEX Technology for $434 million; establishes Challenger Ventures |
| 2016 | Founds Genki Forest with **30 people** — all from the tech industry, none with beverage experience |
| 2016 | First product batch is destroyed — team destroys RMB 5 million of inventory at an additional cost of RMB 1 million; company survives on loans from Challenger Ventures |
| 2016 | Launches **"Burning Tea" (燃茶)** — a low-calorie, low-sugar oolong tea — via **Weibo crowdfunding**; priced at RMB 6/bottle (premium for bottled tea) |
| 2017 | Team's daily routine: search Weibo for brand mentions, respond to every customer comment — building relationships with zero marketing budget |
| 2018 | Launches **sparkling water** (零卡气泡水) — zero-calorie, zero-sugar, in distinctive white bottles; sales in months surpass what Burning Tea achieved in over a year |
| 2018 | Uses **erythritol** as sugar substitute — **70x more expensive** than common alternatives (xylitol, aspartame) — justifies premium pricing through health positioning |
| 2019 | Shanghai sales reach **67% of Coca-Cola's** level |
| 2019 | Launches milk-powdered drink with jasmine/Assam tea extracts and Xiancha (纤茶) based on traditional Chinese medicine ingredients |
| 2020 | Shanghai sales climb to **92% of Coca-Cola's** |
| 2020 | Team grows to **3,000+ employees** (from 30 in 2016) |
| 2020 | Begins building **five proprietary factories** across China to solve production capacity bottleneck |
| 2020 | Tang states that **95% of Genki Forest's planned products have yet to be launched** |
| 2021 | Raises **$6 billion** in funding; annual sales reach ~RMB 7 billion |
| 2021 | Uses KOL marketing with top Chinese influencers (Li Jiaqi, Viya) on Xiaohongshu and Douyin |
| 2022 | Targets exceeding RMB 10 billion in sales; products available in **40+ countries** including US, Japan, Australia, UK |
| 2022 | Five factories fully operational — annual production capacity exceeds **5 billion bottles** |

## Key Research Findings

- **Burning Tea customer feedback (2017):** Customers wanted more variety and bolder flavors.
- **Sugar-free positioning** resonated most strongly with early adopters.
- This feedback directly informed the development of the sparkling water line (2018), which surpassed Burning Tea's sales in months.
