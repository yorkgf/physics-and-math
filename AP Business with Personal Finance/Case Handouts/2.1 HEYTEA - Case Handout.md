# 2.1 HEYTEA (喜茶) — Marketing to Customers

> **Topic:** 2.1 Marketing to Customers
> **Company:** HEYTEA (喜茶)

---

## Background

**HEYTEA (喜茶)** is a Chinese premium tea drink brand founded in 2012 in Jiangmen, Guangdong Province. The company pioneered the **"new-style tea"** category by introducing cheese-fruit tea and fresh-ingredient drinks that transformed tea from a traditional beverage into a lifestyle product. HEYTEA operates over **3,000 stores** across China and has expanded internationally to Singapore, the United Kingdom, Australia, and the United States. The company was valued at approximately **$9 billion** in 2022.

HEYTEA's success is not built on traditional advertising. Instead, the brand relies on **social media virality, data-driven personalization, and cultural collaborations** to reach and retain customers. As of 2023, HEYTEA has **63 million registered members** through its mini-program, giving it one of the richest customer databases in the Chinese beverage industry.

**Target customer profile:** 20–30 years old, predominantly female, concentrated in tier-1 and tier-2 cities. These customers are not simply buying a drink — they are buying an **experience**. HEYTEA's typical customer waits 30–60 minutes in line, takes a photo of the cup, and posts it on WeChat Moments or Xiaohongshu before taking the first sip. The purchase is driven by **social check-in (打卡), lifestyle display, aesthetic experience, and trend-seeking** — not thirst.

## Key Events

| Year | Event |
|---|---|
| **2012** | Founded as "Royal Tea" (皇茶) in Jiangmen, Guangdong; later rebranded to HEYTEA (喜茶) |
| **2016** | Raises Series A funding; begins aggressive expansion into tier-1 cities (Shenzhen, Guangzhou, Shanghai, Beijing) |
| **2017** | Long queues at Shanghai stores go viral on social media — the brand becomes a cultural phenomenon; lines reportedly reach 3–4 hours |
| **2018** | Launches **HEYTEA GO (喜茶GO)** mini-program for mobile ordering — the brand begins collecting full behavioral data (order history, flavor preferences, frequency, location, time of day) |
| **2019** | Store count exceeds 300; international expansion begins (Singapore); launches membership program with points, birthday vouchers, and early access to new products |
| **2020** | Accelerates digitization — many stores become **mini-program only** (no cash accepted); data-driven product development becomes core strategy |
| **2021** | Store count exceeds 800; expands to UK and Australia; valued at approximately $6 billion |
| **2022** | Valued at approximately **$9 billion**; store count exceeds 2,000; expands to the United States |
| **May 2023** | **Fendi x HEYTEA collaboration** — a 19-yuan drink with Fendi branding goes viral; "your first Fendi" becomes a social media meme; first day sells out at many locations; massive coverage on WeChat, Xiaohongshu, and Douyin. This was NOT a traditional ad campaign — it was a **social media-driven cultural event** |
| **2023** | Reaches **63 million registered members**; store count exceeds 3,000; regular collaborations with brands like Oatly, Chinese liquor brands, and local artists continue to drive social media engagement |

## Questions

**Q1.** HEYTEA's Fendi collaboration generated millions of social media impressions without a traditional ad campaign. Marketing encompasses more than advertising, explain why the Fendi collaboration qualifies as marketing even though HEYTEA did not pay for ad placement. What made customers voluntarily share the collaboration on social media?

**Q2.** HEYTEA's HEYTEA GO mini-program captures order history, flavor preferences, visit frequency, location, and time of day for 63 million members. Marketers use data collection tools such as click-tracking, subscriber lists, and social media monitoring, explain how HEYTEA uses each of these three data collection methods. Which method provides the most actionable data for product development, and why?

**Q3.** HEYTEA's target customer is described as a young urban woman (age 20–30, tier-1/2 cities) who values social check-in, aesthetics, and trend-seeking. Market segmentation involves dividing customers by demographics, psychographics, and geography, identify which type of segmentation — demographic, psychographic, or geographic — is **most useful** for HEYTEA's marketing strategy. Use specific case facts to justify your answer.

**Q4.** HEYTEA's membership program offers points, birthday vouchers, early access to new products, and member-exclusive drinks. Businesses use tactics like personalized service, member rewards, and feedback mechanisms to build customer relationships, evaluate which of these tactics is most effective at increasing customer lifetime value (LTV). How does HEYTEA's frequent launch of new products (seasonal, limited-edition, collaborations) reinforce these relationship tactics?

**Q5.** The Fendi x HEYTEA collaboration generated massive organic social media reach at a fraction of the cost of a traditional ad campaign. Customer acquisition cost measures the cost of gaining a new customer, explain how this collaboration kept CAC low. Compare this to what CAC might look like if HEYTEA had tried to achieve the same reach through paid advertising on WeChat, Xiaohongshu, and Douyin.

**Q6.** HEYTEA's mini-program-only stores require customers to order through the app, which collects location, order history, taste preferences, and visit frequency. Marketers must balance data collection with customer privacy, identify the specific privacy risks this creates for HEYTEA's 63 million members. Should HEYTEA be required to offer a cash-payment alternative for customers who do not want to share their data? Justify your reasoning.
