# 2.1 HEYTEA (喜茶) — Case Handout

> **Topic 2.1 Marketing to Customers** · Read before class. Be ready to discuss.

---

## Background

**HEYTEA (喜茶)** is a Chinese premium tea drink brand founded in 2012 in Jiangmen, Guangdong Province. The company introduced cheese-fruit tea and fresh-ingredient drinks, establishing the "new-style tea" category. HEYTEA operates over **3,000 stores** across China and has expanded internationally to Singapore, the United Kingdom, Australia, and the United States. The company was valued at approximately **$9 billion** in 2022.

HEYTEA does not rely on traditional advertising. The brand uses **social media, data-driven personalization, and cultural collaborations** to reach and retain customers. As of 2023, HEYTEA has **63 million registered members** through its mini-program.

**Target customer profile:** 20-30 years old, predominantly female, concentrated in tier-1 and tier-2 cities. HEYTEA's typical customer waits 30-60 minutes in line, takes a photo of the cup, and posts it on WeChat Moments or Xiaohongshu before taking the first sip. The purchase is driven by **social check-in (打卡), lifestyle display, aesthetic experience, and trend-seeking**.

## Key Events

| Year | Event |
|---|---|
| **2012** | Founded as "Royal Tea" (皇茶) in Jiangmen, Guangdong; later rebranded to HEYTEA (喜茶) |
| **2016** | Raises Series A funding; begins expansion into tier-1 cities (Shenzhen, Guangzhou, Shanghai, Beijing) |
| **2017** | Long queues at Shanghai stores generate social media attention; lines reportedly reach 3-4 hours |
| **2018** | Launches **HEYTEA GO (喜茶GO)** mini-program for mobile ordering; collects full behavioral data (order history, flavor preferences, frequency, location, time of day) |
| **2019** | Store count exceeds 300; international expansion begins (Singapore); launches membership program with points, birthday vouchers, and early access to new products |
| **2020** | Many stores become **mini-program only** (no cash accepted); data-driven product development becomes core strategy |
| **2021** | Store count exceeds 800; expands to UK and Australia; valued at approximately $6 billion |
| **2022** | Valued at approximately **$9 billion**; store count exceeds 2,000; expands to the United States |
| **May 2023** | **Fendi x HEYTEA collaboration** — a 19-yuan drink with Fendi branding; first day sells out at many locations; coverage on WeChat, Xiaohongshu, and Douyin |
| **2023** | Reaches **63 million registered members**; store count exceeds 3,000; collaborations with brands like Oatly, Chinese liquor brands, and local artists continue |
