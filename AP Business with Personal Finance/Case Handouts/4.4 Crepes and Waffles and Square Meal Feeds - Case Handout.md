## Background: Crepes & Waffles

Crepes & Waffles is a restaurant chain founded in 1980 by Beatriz Mejía Arango and Eduardo Macías Tofiño in Medellín, Colombia. The company specializes in crepes, waffles, salads, and beverages in the casual dining segment. Restaurants are located primarily in shopping centers and malls, targeting middle-class families, young professionals, and shoppers.

Approximately 70% of Crepes & Waffles' workforce are single mothers — a deliberate hiring model that provides stable income, benefits, training, and career advancement. The company operates 80+ restaurants across Colombia, Panama, Mexico, Ecuador, Peru, Chile, and Spain, employing more than 3,500 people.

## Background: Square Meal Feeds

Square Meal Feeds is a livestock and pet feed manufacturer founded in 2016 by Jake Thompson and Sarah Nguyen in Stillwater, Oklahoma. The company produces customized, nutritionist-formulated feed blends for cattle, horses, poultry, goats, and dogs. Instead of selling generic feed, Square Meal analyzes each customer's animals' nutritional requirements and formulates a custom blend.

Square Meal operates from a 15,000 sq ft production facility and sources grain from local Oklahoma farmers. The company serves 1,200 customers across Oklahoma, Kansas, Texas, and Arkansas — primarily small and medium-sized farms. Large commodity feed companies (Cargill, Purina, Land O'Lakes) have recently begun launching their own "custom" feed blends.
