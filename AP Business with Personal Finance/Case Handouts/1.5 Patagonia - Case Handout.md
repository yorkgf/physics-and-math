## 1. Company Introduction

**Patagonia** is an American outdoor clothing and gear company known for its commitment to environmental activism and ethical business practices.

- **Founded:** 1973 by **Yvon Chouinard** in Ventura, California
- **Product:** Outdoor clothing, climbing gear, camping equipment, and food products — all designed with environmental responsibility
- **Founder background:** Chouinard was a lifelong climber and environmentalist who started by forging his own climbing pitons. He built the company around the idea that business could be a force for environmental good.
- **Industry:** Outdoor apparel and gear

Patagonia is frequently cited as one of the most mission-driven for-profit companies in the world. In September 2022, Chouinard transferred ownership of the company to a nonprofit and a trust, effectively dedicating all future profits to fighting climate change.

## 2. Key Events

| Year | Event |
|---|---|
| **1973** | Yvon Chouinard founds Patagonia; begins selling climbing gear from the back of his car |
| **1980s** | Company expands into outdoor clothing; adopts organic cotton and recycled materials |
| **1996** | Switches 100% of its cotton line to **organic cotton**, despite higher costs and supply chain challenges |
| **2002** | Launches **"Footprint Chronicles"** — publicly tracking and disclosing the environmental impact of its own supply chain, including failures |
| **2011** | Runs a full-page Black Friday ad in the New York Times: **"Don't Buy This Jacket"** — telling consumers to buy less and reduce waste |
| **2012** | Launches **Worn Wear** program — encouraging customers to repair and reuse clothing instead of buying new; offers **free lifetime repairs** |
| **2013** | Files as a **Benefit Corporation** — legally committing to consider environmental and social impact alongside profit |
| **2022 (Sept)** | Chouinard **transfers ownership**: 98% of shares to **Holdfast Collective** (a nonprofit fighting climate change), 2% to a trust ensuring mission enforcement. All future profit goes to environmental causes. Chouinard's public statement: **"Earth is now our only shareholder."** |

**Financial impact of key decisions:**
- The 1996 organic cotton switch increased raw material costs by an estimated **50%**, and Patagonia had to discontinue **one-third of its product line** because organic cotton suppliers could not produce certain weaves.
- The "Don't Buy This Jacket" campaign (2011) ran on Black Friday; despite the anti-consumption message, Patagonia's revenue grew approximately **30% the following year** (from ~$540M to ~$700M).
- The Worn Wear program encourages repair over replacement; Patagonia operates the **largest garment repair facility in North America**.
