# 3.7 DK Coffee Lab — The Balance Sheet and Net Worth

> **Topic 3.7 The Balance Sheet and Net Worth** · Read before class. Be ready to discuss.

---

## Background

This case continues the story of **DK Coffee Lab**, the specialty coffee roaster and cafe founded by **Daniel Kim** in Portland, Oregon. (See the 3.4 and 3.5 handouts for the startup and financing history.)

After evaluating his funding options in mid-2025, Daniel made the following decisions:

| Decision | Outcome |
|---|---|
| SBA bank loan | **Accepted** — $100,000 at 10.5% interest, 5-year term, starting October 2025 |
| Michael Torres (friend loan) | **Accepted** — $50,000 at 8% interest, 3-year term, starting October 2025 |
| Sarah Park (angel investor) | **Declined** — Daniel decided not to give up equity; he wanted to retain full ownership |
| Entity conversion | **Completed** — DK Coffee Lab converted from an LLC to a **C-corporation** in July 2025 |

Daniel combined the $150,000 in loans with $95,000 of his own funds ($70,000 retained earnings + $25,000 personal investment) to fund all three expansion projects:

| Project | Cost | Status by Dec 2025 |
|---|---|---|
| Cafe #4 (Pearl District) | $85,000 | **Open** — launched November 2025 |
| E-commerce website | $60,000 | **Live** — soft-launched December 2025; first orders shipping January 2026 |
| Roasting capacity upgrade (60 kg roaster) | $75,000 | **Operational** — installed November 2025 |

As of **December 31, 2025**, DK Coffee Lab operates **four cafes**, a roasting facility, and an e-commerce platform. The company employs **36 people** and is projecting **$2.4 million in annual revenue for 2026** (up from $1.8M in 2025, driven by Cafe #4 and e-commerce).

Daniel's accountant has prepared the company's first **balance sheet as a C-corporation** — dated December 31, 2025.

## Questions

**1. Respond to parts A, B, and C.**

DK Coffee Lab's balance sheet shows $365,500 in assets, $180,000 in liabilities, and $185,500 in owners' equity.

(A) Explain what each side of the balance sheet equation represents.

(B) If Daniel pays off $20,000 of the bank loan using cash, calculate the new values for total assets, total liabilities, and owners' equity.

(C) Explain why the equation still balances after the transaction in part B.

---

**2. Respond to parts A, B, and C.**

(A) Rank DK Coffee Lab's assets from most easily converted to cash to least easily converted to cash.

(B) Explain why the ability to quickly convert assets to cash matters for a business that must pay weekly expenses like wages and supplies.

(C) Identify which of DK Coffee Lab's long-term assets would be the hardest to convert to cash and explain why.

---

**3. Respond to parts A, B, and C.**

(A) Classify each of the following as a current asset, long-term asset, or intangible asset: (i) $62,000 in cash, (ii) a commercial espresso machine, (iii) the "DK Coffee Lab" trademark, (iv) $38,500 in wholesale invoices not yet paid, (v) the roasting facility's plumbing and electrical build-out.

(B) Explain why assets are listed in a specific order on the balance sheet rather than at random.

(C) Describe what the order of listing tells a reader about the company's financial position.

---

**4. Respond to parts A, B, and C.**

DK Coffee Lab has $55,800 in current liabilities and $124,200 in long-term liabilities. The $100,000 bank loan appears split between both categories.

(A) Explain the difference between current and long-term liabilities.

(B) Explain why the bank loan is split across both categories on the balance sheet.

(C) Calculate how much Daniel must pay toward his total debt obligations (bank loan + friend loan) within the next 12 months, using the data from the liabilities table.

**5. Respond to parts A, B, and C.**

DK Coffee Lab's current assets are $134,500 and current liabilities are $55,800. Total liabilities are $180,000 and total assets are $365,500.

(A) Calculate DK Coffee Lab's working capital and current ratio. Show your work.

(B) Calculate the debt-to-asset ratio and explain what the result means for DK Coffee Lab's financial risk.

(C) Describe one specific daily operation at DK Coffee Lab that depends on having sufficient working capital.
