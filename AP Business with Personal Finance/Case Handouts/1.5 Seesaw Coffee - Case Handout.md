# Seesaw Coffee — Case Handout

> **Topic 1.5 Vision** · Read before class. Be ready to discuss.

---

## 1. Company Introduction

**Seesaw Coffee** was a Chinese specialty coffee brand, once considered one of the pioneers of China's "third wave" coffee movement.

- **Founded:** 2012 in **Shanghai**, China
- **Product:** Specialty coffee drinks, curated café experience, and coffee-related retail products
- **Founder:** Zuo Zuojun (左佐君)
- **Industry:** Specialty coffee / Food & Beverage

Seesaw built its identity around **craft, innovation, and community** — positioning itself as a premium alternative to mass-market chains like Starbucks and later, budget chains like Luckin Coffee. The brand attracted venture capital funding and expanded aggressively into major cities across China.

---

## 2. Key Events

| Year | Event |
|---|---|
| **2012** | Seesaw Coffee opens its first store in Shanghai; positions itself as a specialty coffee pioneer |
| **2012–2017** | Builds reputation for quality beans, skilled baristas, and curated café environments; develops a loyal following among urban professionals |
| **2017–2021** | Receives venture capital funding; begins **aggressive expansion** into multiple Chinese cities; store count grows rapidly |
| **2023** | **Price war** erupts in China's coffee market — Luckin Coffee and Cotti Coffee launch **9.9-yuan coupons** (~$1.35 USD per cup) |
| **2023–2024** | Seesaw responds by launching its own **9.9-yuan promotions** to retain customers; margins collapse because specialty beans and trained baristas cost far more than 9.9 yuan per cup |
| **2024** | Reports of delayed supplier payments, employee salary arrears, and store closures surface |
| **2024–2025** | Mass store closures across China; debts remain unpaid; company enters **bankruptcy/liquidation** |

## Questions

**Q1.** Seesaw's vision was: *"Bring specialty coffee into everyone's daily life."* Is this a **vision statement** or a **mission statement** ? Apply the test: does it describe a **future worth wanting** (vision), or does it describe **what the business does, for whom, and how** (mission)?

**Q2.** Seesaw's core values included **craft**. At what specific point in the company's history did the business **stop testing that value at real decisions**? What did the 9.9-yuan promotion tell customers about Seesaw's commitment to craft?

**Q3.** Seesaw faced pressure from **venture capital investors** to expand and from **discount competitors** to lower prices. Identify these as **internal or external stakeholders** and explain how each influenced decisions that conflicted with the company's stated values.

**Q4.** Compare Seesaw's outcome with **Patagonia's** outcome (see the Patagonia handout). Both companies had stated values. Both faced pressure to compromise. What is **one specific difference** in how each company responded, and how does that difference explain their different outcomes?

**Q5.** Using the **organization decision tree** (, 1.5.B.2), classify Seesaw Coffee. Was it a **business**, a **social enterprise**, or a **nonprofit**? Explain your answer with evidence from the case.
