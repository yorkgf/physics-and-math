# Seesaw Coffee — Case Handout

> **Topic 1.5 Vision** · Read before class. Be ready to discuss.

---

## Background

Seesaw Coffee was a Chinese specialty coffee brand founded in 2012 in Shanghai by Zuo Zuojun (左佐君). The company positioned itself as a pioneer of China's "third wave" coffee movement — emphasizing craft, skilled baristas, and curated café environments. Seesaw attracted a loyal following among urban professionals and received venture capital funding beginning in 2017.

Seesaw's stated purpose was: "Bring specialty coffee into everyone's daily life." The brand expanded aggressively into multiple Chinese cities between 2017 and 2021. However, in 2023, a price war erupted in China's coffee market — Luckin Coffee and Cotti Coffee launched 9.9-yuan coupons (~$1.35 USD per cup). Seesaw responded by launching its own 9.9-yuan promotions to retain customers. Because specialty beans and trained baristas cost far more than 9.9 yuan per cup, margins collapsed.

By 2024, reports surfaced of delayed supplier payments, employee salary arrears, and store closures. The company entered bankruptcy/liquidation in 2024–2025.

**1. Respond to parts A, B, and C.**

Seesaw Coffee's stated purpose was: "Bring specialty coffee into everyone's daily life."

(A) Describe what this statement tells you about what Seesaw's leadership considered most important.

(B) Explain how the 9.9-yuan promotion conflicted with this stated purpose.

(C) Evaluate whether Seesaw's actions between 2023 and 2024 were consistent with its stated purpose.

**2. Respond to parts A, B, and C.**

Seesaw built its brand around craft — quality beans, skilled baristas, and curated café environments.

(A) Identify the specific costs that made Seesaw's products more expensive to produce than those of budget coffee chains.

(B) Explain why Seesaw could not match the 9.9-yuan prices of competitors without sacrificing the qualities that defined its brand.

(C) Recommend one specific strategy Seesaw could have used to respond to the price war without abandoning its commitment to craft.

**3. Respond to parts A, B, and C.**

Seesaw received venture capital funding and expanded aggressively into multiple Chinese cities between 2017 and 2021.

(A) Identify the external funding source Seesaw used to support its expansion.

(B) Explain how pressure from investors to grow quickly may have contributed to the company's difficulties.

(C) Predict what would have happened to Seesaw if the company had expanded more slowly and focused on profitability rather than growth.

**4. Respond to parts A, B, and C.**
