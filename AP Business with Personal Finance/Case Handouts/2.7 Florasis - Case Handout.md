# 2.7 Florasis (花西子) — Case Handout

> **Topic 2.7 Promotion and Marketing Communications** · Read before class. Be ready to discuss.

---

## Background

Florasis (花西子) is a Chinese cosmetics brand founded in 2017 by **Hua Mantian** (花满天, real name Wu Chenglong 吴成龙), a former marketing executive at the cosmetics company 百雀羚. The brand name "花西子" combines "花" (flower) and "西子" (a classical nickname for the legendary beauty Xi Shi from ancient China).

Florasis positioned itself around **"东方美学" (Eastern aesthetics)** — blending traditional Chinese art, culture, and heritage with modern cosmetics. Every product is designed with Chinese cultural motifs: carved lipsticks inspired by traditional relief art, powder compacts with lotus and phoenix designs, and packaging featuring embroidery patterns.

From 2017 to 2020, Florasis grew from zero to approximately **30 billion RMB (~$4.2 billion)** in annual transaction volume, becoming one of China's top cosmetics brands.

## Key Events

| Year | Event |
|---|---|
| 2017 | **Hua Mantian** founds Florasis in Hangzhou, China; launches with "Eastern aesthetics" positioning |
| 2017 | First products feature traditional Chinese design motifs — carved lipsticks, floral compacts |
| 2018 | Partners with **Li Jiaqi (李佳琦)** for livestream selling on Taobao Live — Li Jiaqi's demos of Florasis lipsticks drive sales |
| 2019 | Florasis becomes one of the **top-selling cosmetics brands** on Tmall during Double 11 (Singles' Day) |
| 2019 | Launches the **"Miao Impression" (苗族印象)** collection — products inspired by Miao ethnic minority silver craftsmanship; generates PR coverage |
| 2020 | Enters the **Japanese market** — products sold on Amazon Japan with "Chinese beauty" positioning; Japanese media covers the brand |
| 2020 | Transaction volume reaches approximately **30 billion RMB** (~$4.2B) |
| 2021 | Partners with **Dunhuang Research Academy** for a limited-edition collection inspired by Dunhuang cave murals |
| 2021 | Expands to **Southeast Asia** (Thailand, Malaysia, Philippines) through Shopee and Lazada |
| 2022 | Enters the **US market** through Amazon and its own DTC website |
| 2022 | Runs billboard ads in **Times Square, New York** |
| 2023 | **79-yuan eyebrow pencil controversy**: Li Jiaqi responds to a viewer questioning the price by saying "Have you worked hard enough? Have your wages gone up?" — social media backlash damages both Li Jiaqi's and Florasis's reputation |
| 2023 | Florasis issues a **PR statement** addressing the controversy; begins to diversify away from over-reliance on Li Jiaqi |
| 2024 | Continues international expansion; launches in **Europe**; builds out KOC (Key Opinion Consumer) strategy to reduce KOL dependency |

## Florasis Marketing — Key Facts

### Direct Marketing Examples

| Channel | Detail |
|---------|--------|
| WeChat mini-program | Personalized product recommendations based on purchase history |
| Customer messages via WeChat | Birthday offers, loyalty rewards, new product alerts |
| Email campaigns | Targeted at international customers |

### Platform Strategy

| Platform | What It Enables |
|----------|----------------|
| Xiaohongshu (小红书) | Product discovery, KOC (Key Opinion Consumer) reviews |
| Douyin (抖音) | Short video content, livestream selling |
| WeChat | Customer retention, personalized DTC (direct-to-consumer) |
| Taobao/Tmall | Transaction data, customer demographics |

### Sales Promotion

Florasis runs limited-time discount bundles during major shopping festivals (e.g., Singles' Day/Double 11, 618), offers free sample gifts with purchase, and releases seasonal gift sets at promotional prices.
