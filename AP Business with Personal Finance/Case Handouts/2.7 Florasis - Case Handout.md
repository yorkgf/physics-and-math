# 2.7 Florasis (花西子) — Case Handout

**Topic:** 2.7 Promotion and Marketing Communications

---

## Background

Florasis (花西子) is a Chinese cosmetics brand founded in 2017 by **Hua Mantian** (花满天, real name Wu Chenglong 吴成龙), a former marketing executive at the cosmetics company百雀羚. The brand name "花西子" combines "花" (flower) and "西子" (a classical nickname for the legendary beauty Xi Shi from ancient China).

Florasis positioned itself around **"东方美学" (Eastern aesthetics)** — blending traditional Chinese art, culture, and heritage with modern cosmetics. Every product is designed with Chinese cultural motifs: carved lipsticks inspired by traditional relief art, powder compacts with lotus and phoenix designs, and packaging featuring embroidery patterns.

From 2017 to 2020, Florasis grew from zero to approximately **30 billion RMB (~$4.2 billion)** in annual transaction volume, becoming one of China's top cosmetics brands. The company achieved this through a marketing strategy that used every tool in the promotional mix — and pioneered new forms of digital marketing.

## Key Events

| Year | Event |
|------|-------|
| 2017 | **Hua Mantian** founds Florasis in Hangzhou, China; launches with "Eastern aesthetics" positioning |
| 2017 | First products feature traditional Chinese design motifs — carved lipsticks, floral compacts |
| 2018 | Partners with **Li Jiaqi (李佳琦)** for livestream selling on Taobao Live — Li Jiaqi's passionate demos of Florasis lipsticks drive massive sales |
| 2019 | Florasis becomes one of the **top-selling cosmetics brands** on Tmall during Double 11 (Singles' Day) |
| 2019 | Launches the **"Miao Impression" (苗族印象)** collection — products inspired by Miao ethnic minority silver craftsmanship; generates massive PR coverage |
| 2020 | Enters the **Japanese market** — products sold on Amazon Japan with "Chinese beauty" positioning; Japanese media covers the brand as a cultural phenomenon |
| 2020 | Transaction volume reaches approximately **30 billion RMB** (~$4.2B) |
| 2021 | Partners with **Dunhuang Research Academy** for a limited-edition collection inspired by Dunhuang cave murals — major PR event |
| 2021 | Expands to **Southeast Asia** (Thailand, Malaysia, Philippines) through Shopee and Lazada |
| 2022 | Enters the **US market** through Amazon and its own DTC website |
| 2022 | Runs billboard ads in **Times Square, New York** — media advertising for international brand awareness |
| 2023 | **79-yuan eyebrow pencil controversy**: Li Jiaqi responds to a viewer questioning the price by saying "Have you worked hard enough? Have your wages gone up?" — massive social media backlash damages both Li Jiaqi's and Florasis's reputation |
| 2023 | Florasis issues a **PR statement** addressing the controversy; begins to diversify away from over-reliance on Li Jiaqi |
| 2024 | Continues international expansion; launches in **Europe**; builds out KOC (Key Opinion Consumer) strategy to reduce KOL dependency |

## 3. Florasis Marketing — Key Facts

### Direct Marketing Examples

| Channel | Detail |
|---------|--------|
| WeChat mini-program | Personalized product recommendations based on purchase history |
| Customer messages via WeChat | Birthday offers, loyalty rewards, new product alerts |
| Email campaigns | Targeted at international customers |

### Platform Strategy

| Platform | What It Enables |
|----------|----------------|
| Xiaohongshu (小红书) | Product discovery, KOC (Key Opinion Consumer) reviews |
| Douyin (抖音) | Short video content, livestream selling |
| WeChat | Customer retention, personalized DTC (direct-to-consumer) |
| Taobao/Tmall | Transaction data, customer demographics |

### Sales Promotion

Florasis runs limited-time discount bundles during major shopping festivals (e.g., Singles' Day/Double 11, 618), offers free sample gifts with purchase, and releases seasonal gift sets at promotional prices.

## Questions

**1. Promotional Mix Tools**
Florasis uses all five tools in the promotional mix. For each tool, give one specific example of how Florasis uses it and explain what the tool achieves that the others cannot.

**2. Personal Selling at Scale**
Li Jiaqi's livestreams reach millions of viewers simultaneously. Is this still personal selling, or has it become media advertising? Explain using the CED's definitions.

**3. Consequential vs. Routine Purchases**
A customer watches Li Jiaqi's livestream and buys a Florasis lipstick for the first time (consequential purchase). The same customer later reorders the same lipstick through the WeChat mini-program (routine purchase). How do the promotional tools differ for each purchase?

**4. Digital Marketing Advantages**
Florasis uses Xiaohongshu, Douyin, WeChat, and Taobao/Tmall. What can Florasis learn from each platform's data that traditional cosmetics brands (using only TV ads and magazine ads) could never learn?

**5. PR and Cultural Storytelling**
Florasis's PR strategy frames every product launch as a cultural event (Miao collection, Dunhuang collaboration). Why is this more effective than simply running more advertisements?

**6. Diversifying the Promotional Mix**
After the 79-yuan controversy, Florasis began reducing its dependence on Li Jiaqi. What specific steps could Florasis take to diversify its promotional mix and reduce the risk of a single spokesperson damaging the brand?
