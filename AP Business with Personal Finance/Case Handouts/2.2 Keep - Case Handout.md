# Keep — Case Handout

> **Topic 2.2 Consumer Behavior** · Read before class. Be ready to discuss.

---

## Background

Keep is a Chinese fitness and lifestyle app founded in 2014 in Beijing. The company went public on the Hong Kong Stock Exchange in 2023, making it China's first fitness tech IPO. As of its IPO, Keep reported over 300 million registered users and more than 30 million monthly active users.

Keep operates four revenue streams:
- **Online fitness content** — subscription-based access to workout classes and training plans
- **Smart fitness products** — equipment and wearable devices sold under the Keep brand
- **Branded apparel** — athletic clothing and accessories
- **Advertising** — brand partnerships and in-app promotions

Keep's core users are 18-35 year olds in urban Chinese cities, with over 60% female users. Most are office workers with moderate incomes who find expensive gym memberships inaccessible. The brand's core values center on health, self-improvement, and discipline.

The company's core mission is captured in its brand slogan: **"自律给我自由"** — "Self-discipline gives me freedom."

## Key Events

| Year | Event |
|---|---|
| **2014** | Keep founded in Beijing; launches as a free fitness app offering guided home workouts |
| **2015-2017** | Rapid user growth as smartphone penetration in China surges. Online fitness users in China grow from approximately 100 million to a much larger base. Keep introduces streak counters and workout completion badges to drive daily engagement |
| **2018-2020** | Keep expands into smart fitness products (yoga mats, resistance bands, stationary bikes) and branded apparel. Training camps (训练营) and community features such as "Keep Running" local running clubs are introduced |
| **2021-2022** | Keep reaches 300 million registered users. The platform introduces leaderboards, user-generated content sharing to WeChat Moments, and group workout challenges. Premium subscription tiers are formalized at approximately 19 USD/month or 178 USD/year |
| **2023** | Completes IPO on the Hong Kong Stock Exchange. Monthly active users exceed 30 million. Online fitness users in China have grown to approximately 400 million |

## Questions

**1. Respond to parts A, B, and C.**

Before Keep launched, fitness participation in China required a gym membership costing 50-100 USD per month plus commute time and a fixed schedule. By 2023, online fitness users in China had grown to approximately 400 million.

(A) Identify two ways Keep made fitness more accessible to consumers compared to traditional gyms.

(B) Explain how smartphone technology changed both Keep's ability to reach customers and consumers' access to fitness.

(C) Predict what would happen to Keep's user growth if smartphone penetration in China stopped increasing.

**2. Respond to parts A, B, and C.**

Keep's brand slogan is "自律给我自由" — "Self-discipline gives me freedom." The app uses streak counters ("连续打卡天数") and workout completion badges to reinforce user behavior.

(A) Describe how the streak counter feature works in the Keep app.

(B) Explain how a user's self-image as a "self-disciplined person" influences their continued use of the app over time.

(C) Predict what happens to a user's engagement when they break their streak, and explain what this reveals about the power of the streak feature.
