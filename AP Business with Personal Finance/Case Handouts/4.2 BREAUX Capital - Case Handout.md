# 4.2 BREAUX Capital — Evaluating Performance Using KPIs

> **Topic:** 4.2 Evaluating Performance Using KPIs

## Background

**BREAUX Capital** (Building Resources, Empowerment, And Unity for eXcellence) is a fintech startup founded in **2018** by **Sherman Harris** and **Marlon Breaux** in **Baton Rouge, Louisiana**. The company's mission is to **close the wealth gap for Black men in America** by providing culturally relevant financial tools, resources, and community-driven financial literacy programs.

BREAUX Capital operates a **mobile-first financial platform** that combines a digital banking app with a peer-to-peer savings community. Members join "pods" — small groups of 5–10 people who commit to saving together, holding each other accountable, and building financial habits collectively. The platform offers checking and savings accounts (through a partner bank), automated savings tools, financial literacy content, and community events.

As of **2024**, BREAUX Capital has:
- **12,000+ active members** across 15 U.S. states
- **$4.2 million** in total member savings managed through the platform
- **85 pods** (peer savings groups) actively operating
- **28 full-time employees**
- Raised **$3.8 million** in seed funding from investors including Google for Startups, Backstage Capital, and Village Capital

The company generates revenue through three streams: (1) interchange fees on debit card transactions, (2) a premium subscription tier ("BREAUX Plus") at $9.99/month, and (3) partnerships with employers who offer BREAUX Capital as an employee financial wellness benefit.

## Questions

**1. Selecting KPIs**
A KPI is a data point used to measure a business's performance, including progress toward goals and effectiveness of strategy, BREAUX Capital tracks many data points including active members, app downloads, office square footage, NPS, CLV:CAC ratio, and financial literacy course completion rate. (a) Which of these are true KPIs and which are just data points? Explain your criteria. (b) A BREAUX Capital manager proposes adding "number of social media followers" as a KPI. Evaluate whether this should be a KPI using the criteria — does it measure progress toward goals or strategy effectiveness?

**2. Mission-Aligned KPIs**
Managers select KPIs that relate to their stated mission and goals, profitability, and ability to remain competitive and viable, BREAUX Capital's mission is to "close the wealth gap for Black men." Identify the three KPIs from the case that best measure progress toward this mission. Explain why each one connects to the mission. Then identify one KPI that measures profitability and one that measures long-term viability.

**3. Financial Health**
Businesses monitor financial health using KPIs such as revenue, gross profit, gross profit margin, operating profit, COGS, operating expenses, and cash flow, analyze BREAUX Capital's financial health using the Income Statement and Cash Flow data. (a) Calculate the operating profit margin for Q4 2024. (b) With $1,850,000 in cash and a monthly burn rate of $163,000, how many months of runway does BREAUX have? (c) What three actions could management take to extend the runway? Rank them by feasibility.

**4. Customer KPIs**
Businesses measure marketing and sales goals using KPIs such as CAC, CLV, customer satisfaction, customer retention, total sales, and market share, BREAUX Capital's CLV:CAC ratio improved from 2.7:1 in Q1 to 4.1:1 in Q4 2024. (a) Explain what this ratio means in plain language. (b) Which improved more — CAC decreased or CLV increased? Use the data to support your answer. (c) Why is this ratio more useful than looking at CAC or CLV alone?

**5. Operations KPIs**
Businesses measure operations goals using KPIs such as per-unit cost, delivery cost, order accuracy, and on-time delivery percentage, BREAUX Capital's operations KPIs include app uptime (99.8%), customer support response time (2.6 hours), and feature release cycle time (3.5 weeks). (a) Why is "app uptime" important for a fintech company? What would happen if uptime dropped to 95%? (b) BREAUX's support response time improved from 4.2 hours (Q1) to 2.6 hours (Q4). What operational changes might explain this improvement? (c) The CED mentions "per-unit cost" and "order accuracy" as operations KPIs. How would you translate these concepts into KPIs for BREAUX Capital (a software platform, not a manufacturer)?

**6. Internal vs. External Benchmarks**
A benchmark is a reference point; businesses may base benchmarks on internal historical data or external industry standards, BREAUX Capital compares its performance to both its own historical data and fintech industry averages. (a) Give one example of an internal benchmark and one example of an external benchmark from the case. (b) BREAUX's NPS is 72 vs. the fintech industry average of 45. Explain what this means and why it matters. (c) What are the limitations of comparing BREAUX's KPIs to general fintech industry averages? What would be a better external benchmark?

**7. Benchmark-Driven Decisions**
Businesses compare KPI data to selected benchmarks to assess performance against a known standard, review the "Comparing KPIs to Benchmarks" table. (a) Identify the KPI where BREAUX Capital most significantly outperforms the benchmark and the KPI where it most significantly underperforms. (b) Based on these two KPIs, what specific decisions should BREAUX's management team make? (c) If you could only track THREE KPIs going forward, which three would you choose and why? Justify using the benchmarking data.

**8. Interpreting Financial KPIs Together**
, BREAUX Capital's gross profit margin is 70% (healthy) but its operating profit margin is −127% (deeply negative). (a) Explain how a company can have a high gross profit margin but a deeply negative operating profit margin. What does this tell you about the company's spending? (b) A journalist writes: "BREAUX Capital loses money on every customer it serves." Is this statement accurate? Use the financial KPIs to evaluate this claim. (c) At what revenue level would BREAUX need to operate to break even (operating profit = $0)? Show your calculation.

**9. KPIs Across Industries**
KPIs vary based on the characteristics of the business and the industry, compare BREAUX Capital's KPIs to those of a traditional bank (e.g., Bank of America). Identify three KPIs that both businesses would track and three that are unique to each. Explain why the KPIs differ based on the business model.

**10. The CAC Trend**
, BREAUX Capital's CAC decreased from $175 (Q1) to $135 (Q4 2024), while new members per quarter increased from 800 to 1,000. (a) Explain why CAC might decrease as the company grows. (b) If BREAUX increased its marketing budget by 50% next quarter, would you expect CAC to stay at $135, decrease, or increase? Explain your reasoning using the concept of diminishing returns. (c) What is the maximum CAC BREAUX can afford if it wants to maintain a CLV:CAC ratio of at least 3.0:1?

**11. Benchmarking Cash Runway**
, BREAUX Capital has approximately 11 months of cash runway, while the startup best practice benchmark is 18 months. (a) Explain what "cash runway" means and why 18 months is the recommended benchmark. (b) What specific steps should BREAUX's management take to close the 7-month gap? Consider both increasing revenue and reducing costs. (c) If BREAUX raises a $5 million Series A, how would this change the runway calculation? Show your work.
