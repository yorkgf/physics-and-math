## Course Description

AP Business with Personal Finance is an introductory, college-level business and personal finance course. Students explore the business disciplines of entrepreneurship, marketing, finance, accounting, and management through real-world business application, case studies, and project-based learning. In addition, students learn and apply all the National Standards for Personal Financial Education created by the Council for Economic Education and the Jump$tart Coalition for Personal Financial Literacy.

This course is designed to be the equivalent of a one-semester college introduction to business course and a one-semester college personal finance course.

---

## Course Objectives

Upon completion of this course, students will be able to:

1. **Understand business fundamentals** - Explain how businesses create value, operate, compete, and make ethical decisions
2. **Apply marketing concepts** - Analyze how businesses use market research, consumer behavior, and marketing strategies
3. **Master personal finance** - Apply concepts of saving, borrowing, credit, debt, and investing
4. **Analyze business finance** - Interpret financial statements and understand business accounting
5. **Evaluate management strategies** - Use strategic frameworks like SWOT and Porter's Five Forces
6. **Develop professional skills** - Demonstrate communication, collaboration, and decision-making skills

## Course Policies

### Attendance
- Regular attendance is essential for success in this project-based course
- Students are responsible for obtaining notes and assignments when absent
- More than 3 unexcused absences may result in grade deductions

### Late Work
- Assignments submitted late will receive a 10% deduction per day
- Assignments more than 3 days late will receive a maximum of 50%
- Exceptions made for excused absences and emergencies

### Academic Integrity
- All work must be your own
- Collaboration is encouraged for group projects, but individual submissions must be original
- Plagiarism or cheating will result in a zero and possible course failure

### Professional Conduct
- This course simulates professional business environments
- Students are expected to demonstrate professionalism, respect, and collaboration
- Business-appropriate behavior is required during presentations and group work
