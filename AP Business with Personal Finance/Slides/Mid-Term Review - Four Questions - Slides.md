## 四道全班错得最多的选择题

**1.** A tutoring company has no factory and no warehouse, yet it still has a supply chain. Which combination makes up its service supply chain?
**A.** Suppliers, distributors, and retailers　**B.** Raw materials, manufacturing, and inventory
**C.** Advertising, market research, and pricing　**D.** Employees, resources, and delivery systems

**2.** A company's logo is recognized and trusted by customers, who choose it over identical products sold by other businesses. The added financial and competitive value created by that recognition and trust is best described as:
**A.** Product messaging　**B.** A value proposition　**C.** Brand equity　**D.** Market saturation

**3.** A business spends ¥12,000 on marketing and acquires 400 new customers, who will spend ¥150 each over three years. Which statement is correct?
**A.** CAC is ¥12,000, LTV ¥150 → cut marketing
**B.** CAC is ¥30, LTV ¥150, but judge CAC alone since LTV cannot be estimated
**C.** CAC is ¥150, LTV ¥30 → raise advertising
**D.** CAC is ¥30, LTV ¥150 → building strong relationships is profitable

**4.** A fitness app groups users by age, income, and location, and by whether they value convenience and self-improvement, then writes: "Lin, 24, a busy graduate student who exercises before work." Lin's description is best called:
**A.** A market segment　**B.** A purchasing pattern　**C.** A customer profile　**D.** Customer data
