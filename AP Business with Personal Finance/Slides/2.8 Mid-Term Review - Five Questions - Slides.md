## 全班错得最多的五道选择题

**1**　A tutoring company has no factory and no warehouse, yet it still has a supply chain. Which combination makes up its **service** supply chain?
**A.** Suppliers, distributors, retailers　**B.** Raw materials, manufacturing, inventory　**C.** Advertising, market research, pricing　**D.** Employees, resources, delivery systems

**2**　A company's logo is recognized and trusted, so customers choose it over identical products. The added value created by that recognition and trust is:
**A.** Product messaging　**B.** A value proposition　**C.** Brand equity　**D.** Market saturation

**3**　A business spends ¥12,000 on marketing and acquires 400 new customers, who will spend ¥150 each over three years. Which statement is correct?
**A.** CAC ¥12,000, LTV ¥150 → cut marketing
**B.** CAC ¥30, LTV ¥150, but judge CAC alone since LTV cannot be estimated
**C.** CAC ¥150, LTV ¥30 → raise advertising
**D.** CAC ¥30, LTV ¥150 → building strong relationships is profitable

**4**　A fitness app groups users by age, income and location, and by whether they value convenience, then writes: "Lin, 24, a busy graduate student who exercises before work." Lin's description is best called:
**A.** A market segment　**B.** A purchasing pattern　**C.** A customer profile　**D.** Customer data

**5**　A founder wants to know how many students would pay ¥30 a month for a planner app, and why others would not. Which research design fits both questions?
**A.** An experiment for both questions　**B.** Observation of existing students only
**C.** A focus group for both questions　**D.** A survey for "how many" + interviews for "why"

## 写法要纠正 ①：B3 促销战术——只写做法，不点原则

**问题（全班 24/37）**：写成"我们打折 / 我们送赠品 / 我们请明星"，没有说出这是哪个原则，也没有说明它为什么让人想买。

| | 例子 |
|---|---|
| ✗ 现在这样写 | We provide a free repair kit and free testing. ／ We use discounts and hire actors to advertise. |
| ✓ 应该这样写 | **Reciprocity** — 进店送一张免费饮品券（做法）→ 顾客收到好处后产生回报心理（机制）→ 下次优先回来消费（结果）。 |

**知识点**：Cialdini 六原则——**互惠 reciprocity**（收到好处想回报）、**一致性 consistency**（与过去的选择保持一致）、**从众 consensus**（多数人这么做）、**好感 liking**（喜欢的人推荐）、**权威 authority**（专家背书）、**稀缺 scarcity**（数量或时间有限）。

**固定句式**：原则名 → 具体做法 → 它为什么让顾客掏钱。三个要素缺一个，这题只能拿一半分。

## 写法要纠正 ②：B4 市场研究——五个要素只落一个

**问题（14 人没点图表、12 人没写样本量、11 人假设不可测、4 人整题空白）**：只写"we use a survey, and it is unbiased"，看起来答了，实际五个采分点只拿到一个。

| 要素 | 不合格 | 合格 |
|---|---|---|
| ① 假设 | We want to help students manage time. | **We assumed that ≥40% of Grade 11 students would pay ¥30/month** if a shared calendar were included. |
| ② 方法＋理由 | We use a survey. | A **survey** fits because we need a countable %; the "why" needs **interviews** to follow up. |
| ③ 样本 | a small number of students | **86 students in Grades 10–12 (52% female) + 14 parents**, all in our target group |
| ④ 防偏差（两条） | we make it unbiased | ① 中性措辞＋匿名填写（改问题）② 按年级分层随机、跨班发放（改抽样） |
| ⑤ 图表＋洞察 | a chart shows the result | A **stacked bar chart by grade** showed Grade 11 chose the ¥25 bundle most → we priced it at ¥25 |

**知识点**：方法要匹配数据——要"多少"用问卷（定量、大样本）；要"为什么"用访谈或焦点小组（定性、可追问）；要真实行为用实验/观察；要市场规模用二手资料。防偏差必须一条改**问题设计**、一条改**抽样**。

## 写法要纠正 ③：B2 客户画像——只写"目标客户是学生"

**问题（15 人没有画像、6 人两类数据未分工、6 人把隐私风险写成产品/供应链风险）**："Our target customers are students" 只是目标市场，不是画像。

| | 例子 |
|---|---|
| ✗ 现在这样写 | Our target customers are students who want healthy food. |
| ✓ 应该这样写 | **Lin, 24**（人口：年龄、职业/身份）**, a graduate student who exercises before work and values quick, guided sessions**（心理：价值观与生活方式） |

**知识点**
- **人口特征 demographic**：年龄、性别、收入、地区、职业 → 决定"服务谁、定多少钱"
- **心理特征 psychographic**：价值观、兴趣、生活方式 → 决定"产品做成什么样、怎么宣传"
- **画像要 2＋2**：两条人口＋两条心理，最好是一个有名字的虚构个体
- **隐私题三选一**：收集未告知并转卖＝**privacy violation**；被外部窃取＝**data breach**；这两种都要求写出"一条具体做法"
