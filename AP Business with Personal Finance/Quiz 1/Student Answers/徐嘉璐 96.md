## Q1. Briefly describe your product/service. What is your target customer profile, the problem, need, or want your product/service is intended to address? (25Pts)

Our company is called Petpal. Our services are providing walking pets services and feeding services. Many pet owners sometimes have to leave their home for a period of time without bringing their pets with them, for example business families sometimes may have family travel; working youths may travel for business missions; and university students might be too busy to take care of their pets during mid-term exams. We are tending to provide walking and feeding services to moderate the load of pet owners while they are busy dealing their own daily tasks.

## Q2. How or why your product/service creates value for customers? How does your product/service capture the value? (25Pts)

We create the value of taking care of the pets while the owners can have time. We can achieve this value by hiring high-qualified settings and trained them well enough to ensure that our services' qualities. Moreover, we can match the nearest sector in order to shorten the time for our customers to wait. Previously, these pet-owners might have to leave their pets alone at home or call their friends for help. However, with the help of our program, they can eliminate the risks of leaving pets on their own or having social-debt to others. With greater quantifiable services, our customers can be more satisfied with well-paid services.

## Q3. What is your core competency in the business? What is the barrier of entry of your business?

We are very competitive in the pet market because we are using trust-first policy. Many pet-owners worry about pet-care's safety and credibility. However, we are going to offer high-qualified setters and real-time time matching GPS to satisfy our customers and favoring the risks of our own falsifying consumers and setters. The barrier of entry our business is pretty-law because we don't need a practical store. Even though, we will need an app with grand GPS network showing our customers' and setters' location.

## Q4. Considering the market you will enter, with the framework of PESTEL, which are the most important factors? Analyze these factors of your target market, and how will you manage the potential risks?

I believe the most important factors are environment, social, and legal. For environment, our walking service is largely dependent on this factor. If the weather is bad, it is unable for us to walk the pets around in nearby gardens or green place. We might solve this issue by finding in-door green place, but this program is still in process. Due to the social aspects, we need to build a social network in order to connect the pet-maners, pets, and setters. With the help of the network, we can build higher credibility with customers introducing us to their close-friends. We will use such network to earn the trust of our customers and diminish their concerns for unreliability. Our business need to be legally approved and our employees should all be licensed. We need to prevent from hiring staffs who might have violence in their history nor pet-unfriendly background. To ensure we are operating legally, we should first protect the safety of the pets to show we are operating effectively.

### Q4 —— PESTEL 因素与风险管理（25 / 25）

**与提交材料相符之处——三个因素全部来自你们自己的 PESTEL，且每个都带应对：**

- **Environmental（天气）——逐条命中你们的 E 行**：*"If the weather is bad, it is unable for us to walk the pets around"*——对应 *"Dog walking depends on outdoor conditions. Heavy rain, extreme heat… can make walking unsafe"*（风险）；应对"找室内绿地"方向合理，也承认"还在推进中"。
- **Social**：建社交网络连接主人/宠物/照看员、靠熟客介绍建立信誉——对应 S 行（把宠物当家人）和你们自愿交换里的口碑机制。
- **Legal——正是你们 3B 认定的最大风险**（*"My single biggest PESTEL risk: Legal liability and safety"*）：*"employees should all be licensed… prevent hiring staff with violence in their history or pet-unfriendly background… protect the safety of the pets"*——对应 L 行（照看员对宠物伤人/走失/受伤负法律责任）和 3B 的应对（培训、安全流程、每次服务留档）。**因素、方向、应对三者对齐。**
- 你们 3A 排序是 Social > Legal > Technological——你写中的 Social 和 Legal 正是前两位。

**扣分点（−1）：**

- Technological（你们排第三：照片/GPS/时间戳证明服务完成）在 Q4 里没展开——不过它在你的 Q3 里以 GPS 的形式出现过，不算完全缺席。（−1）
