# 3.9 Ethics and Financial Reporting — Vocab

## Core Terms (AP Exam)

| # | Term | Definition | Connects To 
|---|---|---|---
| 1 | **unethical practices in finance** | Include misuse of funds, tax evasion, embezzlement, bribery, lack of transparency, and fraud (including falsifying information on financial statements) | 3.9.A.1 
| 2 | **misuse of funds** | Using company money for unauthorized purposes; an unethical and illegal practice | 3.9.A.1, 3.9.B.1 
| 3 | **tax evasion** | Deliberately underreporting income or overstating deductions to reduce taxes owed; illegal | 3.9.A.1, 3.9.B.1 
| 4 | **embezzlement** | Stealing money from an organization by someone entrusted with access to its funds; illegal | 3.9.A.1, 3.9.A.2, 3.9.B.1

| 6 | **lack of transparency** | Hiding relevant financial information from stakeholders; unethical | 3.9.A.1 
| 7 | **fraud** | Deliberately misrepresenting financial data; can include falsifying information on financial statements; illegal | 3.9.A.1, 3.9.A.3, 3.9.B.1 
| 8 | **falsifying information** | Altering financial statements to portray a financial situation as better or worse than it really is; used to influence stock prices, secure better loan terms, or avoid taxes | 3.9.A.1, 3.9.A.3

## Trap Pairs — Know the Difference

| Pair | How They're Different |
|---|---|
| **embezzlement** vs. **fraud** | Embezzlement = stealing money you have access to (taking cash from the register). Fraud = misrepresenting financial data (falsifying records). Embezzlement is a form of theft; fraud is a form of deception. Both can occur together. |
| **tax evasion** vs. **tax avoidance** | Tax evasion = ILLEGAL (underreporting income, hiding money). Tax avoidance = LEGAL (using deductions, credits, and tax-advantaged accounts to minimize taxes owed). |
| **audit** vs. **internal controls** | An audit = external review of financial records (done by independent firms, typically annually). Internal controls = internal processes to prevent errors and fraud (done by the company itself, continuously). |
| **professional ethics codes** vs. **codes of conduct** | Professional ethics codes = rules set by professional organizations (e.g., CPA Code of Ethics) that apply to all members industry-wide. Codes of conduct = rules set by individual companies that apply to their own employees. |
| **lack of transparency** vs. **fraud** | Lack of transparency = hiding information (omission — not telling the whole truth). Fraud = actively misrepresenting information (commission — telling a falsehood). Both are unethical, but fraud is typically more severely punished. |
| **GAAP** vs. **financial market regulations** | GAAP = accounting standards for HOW to prepare financial statements (format and rules). Financial market regulations = laws governing WHAT must be disclosed and protecting investors from fraud. GAAP tells you how to report; regulations tell you that you must report accurately. |
