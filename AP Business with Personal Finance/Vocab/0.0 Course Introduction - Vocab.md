# 0.0 Course Introduction — Vocabulary

> Key **course & exam** terms. Companion to [[0.0 Course Introduction - Slides]].
> Source: AP Business with Personal Finance CED (2026 Fall).

## The course

| Term | Definition |
|---|---|
| **AP Business with Personal Finance** | An introductory, college-level course in business + personal finance (≈ one-semester college equivalent; no prerequisites). |
| **Career Kickstart** | AP designation for courses built with **industry employers** as well as colleges — equips students for specific careers. |
| **CED** | **Course and Exam Description** — the official College Board document specifying what students must know and do. |
| **Course framework** | The two components of the course: **Course Skills** + **Course Content** (5 units). |

## The 5 skill categories

| Skill | Focus | MC weight |
|---|---|---|
| **Skill 1 — Concept Application** | apply business concepts | 45–55% |
| **Skill 2 — Entrepreneurship** | identify opportunities; build & test ideas | 5–15% |
| **Skill 3 — Decision Making** | evaluate options; choose with evidence | 25–35% |
| **Skill 4 — Communication** | convey ideas & findings | 5–15% |
| **Skill 5 — Collaboration** | work effectively in teams | *(via projects)* |

## The two projects

| Term | Definition |
|---|---|
| **Business Canvas Project** | A modified Business Model Canvas built across **Units 1–4** (6 components + appendix); assessed via the Exam-Day Validation FRQ. |
| **Financial Advisor Project** | A **Unit 5** project giving financial recommendations to a fictional household. |
| **Exam-Day Validation FRQ** | The exam question (FRQ 1) where you pitch your Business Canvas idea, explain your hypothesis testing, and address a viability challenge. |

## The exam (2 h 40 m · 60 MCQ + 4 FRQ)

| Term | Definition |
|---|---|
| **Section I — Multiple-choice (MC)** | 60 set-based questions · 60% · 70 minutes. |
| **Section II — Free-response (FRQ)** | 4 questions · 40% · 90 minutes. |
| **Part IIA** | **Q1 only** — Business Canvas Project Exam-Day Validation (15%, 25 min). |
| **Part IIB** | **Q2** Personal Finance + **Q3** Business Concept Application + **Q4** Business Decision (65 min). |
| **Business Decision FRQ** | Q4 — the largest single FRQ (15%). |

> Units 1–4 are exam-assessed; Unit 5 (personal finance) may be taught before or after. ~20–25% of the exam weaves in personal-finance items.
