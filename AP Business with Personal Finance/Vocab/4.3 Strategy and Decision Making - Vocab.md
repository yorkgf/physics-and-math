# 4.3 Strategy and Decision Making

## Core Terms

| # | Term | Definition | Connects To 
|---|---|---|---
| 1 | Strategy | A plan or approach to achieving a goal; in business, how a company will achieve competitive advantage, fulfill its mission, or increase profits | 4.3.A.1 
| 2 | Competitive advantage | A condition or circumstance that puts a business in a favorable or superior business position over its rivals | 4.3.A.1, 1.3 
| 3 | Tactics | Specific actions or approaches intended to advance the business's strategy | 4.3.A.4 
| 4 | Strategic data | Information about financial performance, customers, competitors, and market trends that businesses gather and track to define, evaluate, and modify strategy | 4.3.A.2 
| 4 | PACED model | A deliberative decision-making process: Problem, Alternatives, Criteria, Evaluate, Decide | 4.3.B.1 
| 5 | Problem (PACED) | The first step — clearly defining the decision to be made or the challenge to address | 4.3.B.1 
| 6 | Alternatives (PACED) | The second step — developing a list of possible options or courses of action | 4.3.B.1 
| 7 | Criteria (PACED) | The third step — establishing the standards or factors by which alternatives will be judged | 4.3.B.1, 4.3.B.2 
| 8 | Evaluate (PACED) | The fourth step — assessing each alternative against the established criteria | 4.3.B.1, 4.3.B.4 
| 9 | Decide (PACED) | The fifth step — selecting the best alternative based on the evaluation | 4.3.B.1 
| 10 | Return on investment (ROI) | A financial metric measuring the additional profit generated by an investment, divided by the cost of the investment | 4.3.B.3 
| 11 | Decision-making criteria | The standards, factors, or benchmarks used to evaluate and compare alternatives | 4.3.B.2 
| 12 | Quantifiable criteria | Measurable, numerical factors such as costs, revenues, profits, and timelines | 4.3.B.2 
| 13 | Intangible criteria | Non-numerical factors such as reputation, mission alignment, employee morale, and brand impact | 4.3.B.2

## Trap Pairs

| Pair | Term A | Term B | Key Difference |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Strategy** | **Tactics** | Strategy = the overall plan ("expand into retail"). Tactics = specific actions to execute the plan ("attend trade show," "hire sales rep"). Strategy is "what and why"; tactics are "how." |
| 2 | **Quantifiable criteria** | **Intangible criteria** | Quantifiable = measurable numbers (ROI, cost, revenue). Intangible = non-numerical (reputation, morale, mission). Both matter in decision making. |
| 3 | **ROI** | **Revenue** | Revenue = total money earned. ROI = the return generated relative to the investment cost. A project can have high revenue but low ROI if the investment was very large. |
| 4 | **Problem** | **Alternatives** (PACED) | Problem = what you're trying to solve. Alternatives = the possible solutions. You must define the problem before developing alternatives. |
| 5 | **Competitive advantage** | **Strategy** | Competitive advantage = the favorable position itself (what you have). Strategy = the plan to achieve or maintain that position (how you get/keep it). |
