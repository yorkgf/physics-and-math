## Trap Pairs — Know the Difference

| Pair | How They're Different |
|------|-----------------------|
| **Personal selling** vs. **Media advertising** | Selling = one-on-one interaction with detailed info for consequential purchases; advertising = one message to a mass audience for routine purchases |
| **Public relations** vs. **Media advertising** | PR builds a favorable image through earned media (press releases, interviews); advertising controls the message through paid media placement |
| **Direct marketing** vs. **Media advertising** | Direct = targeted message sent to specific potential customers; media advertising = same message broadcast to a broad audience simultaneously |
| **Sales promotion** vs. **Personal selling** | Promotion = short-term incentive to drive quick decisions (discounts, coupons); selling = sustained, detailed interaction with a specific buyer |
| **Traditional marketing** vs. **Digital marketing** | Traditional = offline, harder to track individual responses, broad reach; digital = online, enables data collection and personalization at lower cost |
| **Consequential purchase** vs. **Routine purchase** | Consequential = high-involvement, needs personalized selling or detailed info; routine = low-involvement, responds to mass media or direct marketing |

## Promotional Mix at a Glance

| Tool | What It Is | Best For | Example |
|------|-----------|----------|---------|
| **Media advertising** | Paid messages through mass channels (TV, radio, print, billboards) | Reaching a large audience with the same message at the same time | A Super Bowl TV commercial for a new smartphone |
| **Personal selling** | Direct, face-to-face interaction providing detailed info or a demonstration | Consequential purchases that require trust and detailed explanations | A car salesperson walking a buyer through financing options |
| **Sales promotion** | Short-term incentives that encourage quick buying decisions or clear unsold inventory | Driving rapid purchases or moving excess stock | A "buy one, get one free" offer at a grocery store |
| **Direct marketing** | Targeted messages sent directly to many potential customers via mail, email, or flyers | Delivering a tailored message to a wide but specific audience | A pizza chain mailing discount flyers to households in a neighborhood |
| **Public relations** | Activities that build a favorable public image rather than advance a specific sale | Shaping public perception and managing the brand's reputation | A company issuing a press release about its charitable donations |

## Traditional vs. Digital Marketing

| Dimension | Traditional | Digital |
|-----------|------------|---------|
| **Channels** | TV, radio, newspapers, billboards, direct mail | Websites, email, social media, mobile apps |
| **Audience reach** | Broad, general audience with limited targeting | Global reach or precise targeting of specific segments |
| **Personalization** | One-size-fits-all message for the entire audience | Messages tailored to individual customer data and preferences |
| **Cost** | Typically higher production and placement costs | Generally lower costs, especially for small businesses |
| **Data collection** | Minimal ability to track individual customer responses | Enables collecting big data on customer behavior and buying factors |
| **Measurability** | Difficult to measure exact impact (e.g., how many saw a billboard) | Highly measurable through clicks, impressions, conversions, and engagement metrics |
| **Speed of feedback** | Slow — results take weeks or months to assess | Fast — real-time data on campaign performance and customer responses |
| **Interaction** | One-way communication from business to customer | Two-way communication; customers can respond, share, and engage |

## Quick Decision Framework

```
Which promotional tool fits?
│
├─ Customer needs detailed info + demo?
│   → Personal selling (consequential purchases)
│
├─ Customer makes routine, low-effort decisions?
│   → Media advertising or direct marketing
│
├─ Need to clear inventory fast or spike short-term demand?
│   → Sales promotion (discounts, coupons)
│
├─ Want to build long-term brand reputation?
│   → Public relations (press releases, interviews)
│
└─ Want to reach global audience + collect data?
    → Digital marketing (websites, email, social media)
```
