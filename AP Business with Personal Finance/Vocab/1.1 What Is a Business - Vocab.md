# 1.1 What Is a Business — Vocabulary

> Key terms for Topic 1.1. Companion to [[1.1 What Is a Business - Summary]] · [[1.1 What Is a Business - Slides]].
> Definitions aligned to the CED. Running example: the **candy seller**.

## Core terms

| Term | Definition |
|---|---|
| **Business** | An organization or entity that **produces and distributes products** (goods and/or services); can be any size, face-to-face or virtual. |
| **Product** | A good, a service, or both — what a business produces and distributes. |
| **Goods** | Tangible items (a candy bar, a phone, a desk). |
| **Services** | Work performed for compensation (a haircut, a tutoring session, a flight). |
| **Revenue** | Money earned from selling, **before** expenses. |
| **Profit** | What remains after **all** expenses are subtracted. (**Profit = Revenue − Total Cost**) |
| **Customer** | The individual or business that **purchases** a good or service. |
| **Consumer** | The individual who **uses** a good or service, whether or not they bought it. |
| **Need** | Something essential for survival (food, water, shelter, basic healthcare). |
| **Want** | Improves life without being essential (a Netflix subscription, new sneakers). |
| **Market opportunity** | A customer problem, need, or want that a business **might** address. |
| **Problem-solution fit** | The **match** between a customer problem and a product built to solve it. |
| **Value** | The worth or benefit of a product to customers (measured by the **highest price they'd willingly pay**). |
| **Value creation** | When a business provides a product that **responds to** customers' problems, needs, or wants. |
| **Value capture** | When a business charges a **higher price than it cost to produce**. (**Value captured = Price − Cost**) |
| **Value proposition** | A clear statement of how a product solves a customer problem and why choose it over alternatives. |

## Easily confused (trap pairs)

| Pair | The difference |
|---|---|
| **Customer vs. Consumer** | **purchase** vs. **use** (the buyer vs. the user — can be different people). |
| **Value creation vs. Value capture** | benefit **to the customer** vs. profit **to the business**. Create first, **then** capture. |
| **Need vs. Want** | **survival** vs. **improves life**. |
| **Revenue vs. Profit** | **before** expenses vs. **after** all expenses. |
| **Value vs. Price** | what it's **worth to the customer** vs. what they **actually pay**. |

## Must-know formulas

- **Profit = Revenue − Total Cost**
- **Value captured = Price charged − Cost to produce**

> Trap to remember: a scenario showing **popularity + price below cost** = **value creation without value capture** (e.g., MoviePass).
