# 4.1 Management and Leadership

## Core Terms

| # | Term | Definition | Connects To 
|---|---|---|---
| 1 | Management | The process of planning, organizing, leading, and evaluating a business's use of human, financial, and physical resources to meet goals and objectives | 4.1.A.1, 3.3 
| 2 | Planning | The management function of setting goals and determining the actions needed to achieve them | 4.1.A.1, 4.3 
| 3 | Organizing | The management function of arranging resources and tasks to achieve the business's goals | 4.1.A.1, 4.1.A.3 
| 4 | Leading | The management function of motivating, directing, and guiding employees toward achieving goals | 4.1.A.1, 4.1.B.2 
| 5 | Evaluating | The management function of assessing performance against goals and making adjustments | 4.1.A.1, 4.2 
| 6 | Vision | A statement describing what the business aspires to become or achieve in the long term | 4.1.A.3, 4.1.B.2 
| 7 | Mission | A statement describing the business's fundamental purpose — why it exists and whom it serves | 4.1.A.3, 4.1.B.2 
| 8 | Core competency | A unique skill, knowledge, or capability that gives a business a competitive advantage | 4.1.C.2, 4.4.C.3 
| 9 | Outsourcing | Hiring an external company to perform tasks that are not part of the business's core competencies | 4.1.C.2 
| 10 | Compensation | The total pay and benefits an employee receives in exchange for their work | 4.1.D.1, 4.1.D.2 
| 11 | Salary | A fixed annual amount paid to an employee, typically in equal installments regardless of hours worked | 4.1.D.2, 3.4 
| 12 | Commission | A variable payment based on a percentage of sales or deals closed by the employee | 4.1.D.2 
| 13 | Hourly wage | A fixed amount paid per hour of work | 4.1.D.2 
| 14 | Stakeholder | Any individual or group affected by or able to affect the business's decisions | 4.1.B.1, 4.3.B.2 
| 15 | Communication skills | The ability to express ideas clearly, communicate persuasively, listen empathetically, and correctly interpret and respond to feedback | 4.1.B.3 
| 16 | Work arrangement | The type of employment relationship: full-time, part-time, temporary, or contract — chosen based on business needs | 4.1.D.1 
| 17 | Compensation factors | The criteria employers use to determine pay, including education, skills, and productivity | 4.1.D.3

## Trap Pairs

| Pair | Term A | Term B | Key Difference |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Vision** | **Mission** | Vision = what the business wants to become (future state). Mission = why the business exists (current purpose). Vision is aspirational; mission is operational. |
| 2 | **Salary** | **Hourly wage** | Salary = fixed annual amount, same each pay period regardless of hours. Hourly wage = paid per hour worked; varies with hours. |
| 3 | **Commission** | **Profit sharing** | Commission = based on individual sales performance. Profit sharing = based on overall company profits distributed to all eligible employees. |
| 4 | **Core competency** | **Outsourcing** | Core competency = what the business does BEST (keep in-house). Outsourcing = delegating tasks that are NOT core competencies to external providers. |
| 5 | **Management** | **Leadership** | Management = planning, organizing, leading, evaluating (process-oriented). Leadership = articulating vision, motivating people, building teams (people-oriented). A manager may not be a leader; a leader may not be a manager. |
