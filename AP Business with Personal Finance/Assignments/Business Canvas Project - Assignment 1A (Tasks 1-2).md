## The framework — four rules

1. **Customer ≠ consumer** (EK 1.1.A.2). The **customer** pays; the **consumer** uses. They can be the same person or different — say which, and why it matters.
2. **A problem is not a product idea.** Describe what the customer struggles with *today* and why the current solution is unsatisfactory.
3. **Voluntary exchange creates value on both sides** (EK 1.2.A.2): the buyer gets value, the seller gets revenue — that exchange is what a market is.
4. **Three competitive strategies** (EK 1.2.B.5): **differentiation** (better/unique), **cost leadership** (similar product, lower price), **niche** (a specific segment rivals ignore). Barriers to entry protect you — and can block you (EK 1.2.B.6).
