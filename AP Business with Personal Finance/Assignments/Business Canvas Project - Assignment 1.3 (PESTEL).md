## The framework — four rules

1. **PESTEL = six kinds of outside forces** (EK 1.3.A.1): **P**olitical · **E**conomic · **S**ocial · **T**echnological · **E**nvironmental · **L**egal. They decide which businesses are **viable** — able to survive and stay successful in a market over time.
2. **Classify by the source of the force** — not by what it costs or what subject it touches. A solar-panel *subsidy* concerns the environment, but the force acting on the market is a government policy choice → **political**.
3. **Political vs. legal:** a broad policy *choice* (tax, ban, subsidy) is **political**; a specific codified *rule to comply with* (licence, safety code) is **legal**.
4. **The six factors are never equally important.** Name the ones that decide *your* fate and weigh those — "all six matter equally" is always a weak analysis.

> **Strong-answer method:** **Name the factor → state its direction (helps or hurts) → tie it to YOUR business.**
