### B1
**a:** Our production process is using mass production. We prioritize customers who are in work, and customers who are in school. We choose mass production because mass production can produce our laptop holder a large amount in a short time; also our design is not needed to do artisan production. We prioritize customers in school and in work because they use the holders more often.

**b:** Our key suppliers were factories that produce a weight metal. Our supply chain starts at raw materials; the key supplier in this stage were less weight metal factory, and in manufacturing stage our key suppliers are factories with machines to make our product. Next we need a storage room for finished products, so a warehouse is needed. At last we need three shops and online sales for distribution and retail.

**c:** A business weighs the most is quality; quality mattered the most in my decision. One potential risk is the quality of my product might be different because of mass production. My business would manage it by checking frequently on the factory with machines and checks, and clean the machines more frequently.

### C1
