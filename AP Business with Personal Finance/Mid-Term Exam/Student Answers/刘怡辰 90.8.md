### B1
**a:** Our project is a fortune-telling platform which focuses on entertainment-style emotional companionship. We connect our customers with our certified fortune-tellers through our platform. Our targeted customers priorities emotional companionship service, which can help them reduce stress and gain an outlet to be listened. Our core competencies are empathy-first companionship services, which also response quickly to meet users' need.

**b:** As our project is an online app, we don't have physical supply chain. But we have employees (certified fortune-tellers), delivery systems and resources (platform and voice-calling technology). Fortune-tellers generate content and connect/pair with customers through our platform, then deliver the content to our customers.

**c:** Price. If we choose suppliers that are more expensive, it will raise cost, and reduce profit.

### B2
**a:** We use surveys and interviews to collect customers' data; we find that our customers are mostly young people aged from 18–36, and these people usually feel very anxious and need emotional comfort when facing difficulties. ~~There~~ However, this need meets with our services so that we can narrow the range of our targeted customers.

**b:** We found that a part of people just use our app for several days and then quit it; customers who keep interacting with the app shows their consistency. Therefore, we can identify them. Our customer profile: young people aged from 18–36, mostly women, need support and emotional comfort when facing life difficulties, usually feel uncertain and anxious.

**c:** As our project is an online fortune-telling app, many people might think it as fraud. Therefore, we decide to offer emotional companionship services instead of promising certain accurate prediction.

### B3
**a:** The decision is most likely impulsive. Because most of our customers are young people, they are more likely to accept new services like this. Also, when facing sense of uncertainty and anxiety, they may be more impulsive to know a certain answer. Also, nowadays, as technology develops, metaphysics content are spreading widely and being known by more people. It become more popular.

**b:** **Scarcity** and **Authority**. We can limit the time that each fortune-teller can meet with the customers, which gives them a sense of scarcity. Also, we can post the certification of our fortune-tellers to prove ~~that~~ build our credibility, which gives them a sense of authority.

**a:** Whether people are willing to buy such companionship services at a price of 30 yuan per time.

**b:** I conducted a survey because it can collect massive data from wide range of people at one time. The composition of sample is people who lived in big cities and aged from 18–30. Question: whether you are willing to buy such services at a price of 30 yuan per time? (neutral question). Sample include all people of all age. So it is inclusive and not biased.
