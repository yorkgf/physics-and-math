### B1
**a:** Our business project is an artisan production because it doesn't focus on large quantities but focus on personal requirement details and smaller quantities. We find out that there's many need of pet-caring but there's not many companies offer this service existing in the market, so we decide to choose it.

**b:** Key suppliers are the workers or volunteers that gives pet-caring services. Workers → get ~~tests~~ demands from our app → choose one to give service → to consumers get service.

**c:** Quality is the one that mattered most in our supplier choosing decision, because only with high service quality can attract more customers and build our brand awareness as we are a new company in the introduction stage. One risk is if there's not enough workers at first to cover a huge area; we can cooperate with some volunteer groups first — it can not only solve the problem of the lack of workers but also help us to spread the awareness of our brand.

### B2
**a:** We use survey and searching primary source data to collect customer data. We use demographic data to select customers who own pets and are at a working age so that they might be too busy to take good care of their pets all the time. Psychographic data can help us know more about customers' idea on which point of service do they aware more.

**b:** Firstly they have to own a pet, secondly they don't have time to take care of their pets all the time. Age: work age. Location: city center. Psychographic: willing to pay for their pets caring and have constant income.

**c:** Because workers have to enter customers' home to take care of their pets, privacy must be an important thing customers concern about, so we will ask workers to take videos of what they do ~~all alone~~ during their working time.

### B3
**a:** It is most likely rational. They purchase our service mainly because of their personal time schedule is conflict with their pet caring time; they then will concern about the psychological aspect of whether it is worth to purchase our service; it might be affected by social factor because their friends may suggest them if they feel satisfied with our service.

**b:** We will first give a lower price to the new customers to attract them, then set a rule to inspire customers spread our service to more people to gain some tickets for a lower price.

### B4
**a:** We hypothesize that our target customers age group is between 20–30 and 20% of them have the need of our service.

**b:** We did a survey to people who have pets of all age groups, and asked whether they don't have time to take care of their pets sometimes; about one thousand people join in our survey, and we also search the primary source of the main age group of pet owners.

**c:** Bar chart of the amount of people who need our service of different age groups, where we can directly see which age groups needs the most so that we can give advertisement directly to those people.
