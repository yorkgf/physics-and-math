## Analysis

This question requires distinguishing between elastic and inelastic collisions and applying conservation laws.

### Types of Collisions
1. **Perfectly Elastic**: Both momentum AND kinetic energy conserved
   - $m_1v_{1i} + m_2v_{2i} = m_1v_{1f} + m_2v_{2f}$
   - $\frac{1}{2}m_1v_{1i}^2 + \frac{1}{2}m_2v_{2i}^2 = \frac{1}{2}m_1v_{1f}^2 + \frac{1}{2}m_2v_{2f}^2$

2. **Inelastic**: Momentum conserved, kinetic energy NOT conserved
   - Some energy converted to heat, sound, deformation

3. **Perfectly Inelastic**: Objects stick together, maximum kinetic energy loss

Many students confused the conditions or failed to apply both conservation laws correctly.
