### Key Concepts
- Impulse-Momentum: $\vec{F}_{avg}\Delta t = \Delta \vec{p}$
- Embedding = perfectly inelastic collision (objects stick together)
- $F_{avg} = \frac{\Delta p}{\Delta t}$

### Common Mistakes
- Didn't account for direction/sign
- Used wrong initial/final velocities
- Confused with energy instead of momentum
