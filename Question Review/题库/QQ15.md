## Analysis

This question involves interpreting force-time graphs for a system of two carts.

### System Analysis
For two carts interacting:
- Internal forces between carts are equal and opposite (Newton's 3rd Law)
- External forces affect total system momentum
- Total impulse = Total change in momentum

### Graph Interpretation
- Area under F-t curve = Impulse
- For system: Total impulse from external forces = ΔP_system
- Internal forces cancel in momentum calculation for the system

### Key Principle
$$\vec{F}_{ext} = \frac{d\vec{P}_{system}}{dt}$$

The total momentum of a system changes only due to external forces.
