### Key Concepts
- Angular displacement: $\Delta\theta = 2\pi$ for one complete turn
- Angular velocity: $\omega = \frac{\Delta\theta}{\Delta t}$
- $\omega = \frac{2\pi}{T} = 2\pi f$

### Common Mistakes
- Confused angular velocity with frequency
- Incorrect period calculation
