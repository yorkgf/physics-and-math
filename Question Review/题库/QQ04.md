## Analysis

This question tests understanding of Kepler's Third Law and how orbital period changes with radius.

### Key Concept: Kepler's Third Law
$$T^2 \propto r^3$$

If orbital radius doubles:
$$T_2^2 = T_1^2 \cdot \left(\frac{r_2}{r_1}\right)^3 = T_1^2 \cdot 2^3 = 8T_1^2$$
$$T_2 = T_1 \cdot \sqrt{8} = 2.83T_1$$

### Why Students Struggled
- **Answer B (25%)**: Applied $T \propto r$ instead of $T^2 \propto r^3$
- **Answer C (25%)**: Applied $T \propto r^2$ (confused with other relations)
- Only 2 students correctly identified that period increases by factor of $\sqrt{8}$
