### Key Concepts
- $F_s = -kx$
- Series: $\frac{1}{k_{eq}} = \frac{1}{k_1} + \frac{1}{k_2}$ (softer, stretches more)
- Parallel: $k_{eq} = k_1 + k_2$ (stiffer, stretches less)

### Common Mistakes
- Forgot softer spring stretches more for same force
- Didn't identify series vs parallel correctly
