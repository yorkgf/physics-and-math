## Analysis

This problem involves friction and normal forces when a body is pressed against a vertical wall.

### Contact with Vertical Wall
For an object held against a vertical wall with horizontal force P:
- **Normal force**: $N = P$ (horizontal applied force)
- **Weight**: $W = mg$ (downward)
- **Friction force**: $f_s = \mu_s N = \mu_s P$ (upward, preventing sliding)

### Condition to Prevent Sliding
$$f_s \geq mg$$
$$\mu_s P \geq mg$$
$$P \geq \frac{mg}{\mu_s}$$

### Why No One Got It Right?
- **Answer A/E**: Likely made errors in free-body diagram
- Students may have confused the direction of friction
- Need to correctly relate normal force to applied force P

### Key: Normal Force on Vertical Wall
Unlike horizontal surfaces, the normal force on a vertical wall equals the horizontal force applied to press the object against the wall.
