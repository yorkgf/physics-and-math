## Analysis

This question tests understanding of Simple Harmonic Motion dynamics.

### SHM Acceleration-Displacement Relationship
For simple harmonic motion:
$$a = -\omega^2 x$$

This is the **defining characteristic** of SHM.

### What This Means
- Acceleration is **proportional to negative displacement**
- Graph of a vs x is a **straight line through origin with negative slope**
- Slope = $-\omega^2$

### Alternative Form
$$F = ma = -kx$$
$$a = -\frac{k}{m}x$$

Where $\omega^2 = \frac{k}{m}$

### Why 50% Correct?

- **Answer D**: Wrong sign or proportional relationship
- Understanding that a-x graph is LINEAR (not sinusoidal) is key

### Key Distinction
- **a vs x**: Straight line (negative slope)
- **a vs t**: Sinusoidal (cosine for cosine-type SHM)
- **v vs t**: Sinusoidal (sine for cosine-type SHM)
