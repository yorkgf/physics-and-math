## Analysis

This question tests understanding of impulse and the relationship between force and time.

### Impulse-Momentum Theorem
$$\vec{J} = \int \vec{F} \, dt = \Delta \vec{p} = m\vec{v}_f - m\vec{v}_i$$

### Force-Time Graph Interpretation
- **Impulse** = Area under F-t curve
- Average force relationship:
$$\vec{F}_{avg} = \frac{\Delta \vec{p}}{\Delta t}$$
