## Analysis

This orbital mechanics question requires understanding Kepler's laws and properties of elliptical orbits.

### Key Concepts
- **Perihelion**: Closest point to the Sun (or central body)
- **Aphelion**: Farthest point from the Sun
- For ellipse: $r_{perihelion} = a(1-e)$ where $a$ is semi-major axis, $e$ is eccentricity
- **Kepler's Second Law**: Equal areas in equal times → planet moves faster at perihelion

### Common Mistakes
- **Answer A or E**: Likely confused perihelion with aphelion or used wrong formula
- Students choosing wrong answers may not understand ellipse geometry
