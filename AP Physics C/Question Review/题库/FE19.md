# Final Q19 - Velocity and acceleration at maximum elevation

## Original Question
An object is shot vertically upward into the air with a positive initial velocity. Which of the following correctly describes the velocity and acceleration of the object at its maximum elevation?
(A) Velocity: Positive, Acceleration: Positive
(B) Velocity: Zero, Acceleration: Zero
(C) Velocity: Negative, Acceleration: Negative
(D) Velocity: Zero, Acceleration: Negative
(E) Velocity: Positive, Acceleration: Negative

## Answer Key: A
