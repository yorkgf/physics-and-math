# Final Q13 - Total momentum of system after collision

## Original Question
Two pucks moving on a frictionless air table are about to collide, as shown above. The $1.5 \text{ kg}$ puck is moving directly east at $2.0 \text{ m/s}$. The $4.0 \text{ kg}$ puck is moving directly north at $1.0 \text{ m/s}$.
What is the magnitude of the total momentum of the two-puck system after the collision?

(C) $5.0 \text{ kg}\cdot\text{m/s}$

(E) $5.5\sqrt{5} \text{ kg}\cdot\text{m/s}$

### Key Concepts
- $\vec{p}_{total,initial} = \vec{p}_{total,final}$
- Momentum ALWAYS conserved in collisions
- Elastic: both $p$ and $K$ conserved
- Inelastic: only $p$ conserved
