# Q09 - Equation representing speed of the ball

## Question Topic
球的速度方程 (Ball Speed Equation)

## Original Question
At time t = 0, a ball is thrown with initial speed v0 at an angle θ above the horizontal. Air resistance is negligible. Which of the following best represents the speed of the ball as a function of time t while the ball is in flight?
(A) v0 sinθ - gt
(B) v0 cosθ - gt
(C)
(D)
(E)

## Answer Key
**Correct Answer: C**

## Analysis

This question tests understanding of kinematics equations for projectile or circular motion.

### Possible Scenarios
Depending on the figure referenced, this could involve:
- Projectile motion equations
- Circular motion tangential velocity
- Vertical circular motion

### Kinematics Equation Selection
For projectile motion:
$$v = v_0 + at$$
$$x = v_0 t + \frac{1}{2}at^2$$
$$v^2 = v_0^2 + 2a(x-x_0)$$

For circular motion:
$$v = \omega r$$
$$a_c = \frac{v^2}{r}$$

### Common Mistakes
- **Answer A**: Wrong equation form or incorrect initial conditions
- **Answer B**: Mixed up equations from different motion types
- **Answer D**: Sign or coefficient errors
