(A) 1 N•m
(B) 2 N•m

## Analysis

This is the **only question everyone got correct**, demonstrating mastery of torque concepts.

### Torque on a Meterstick
$$\tau = r \times F = rF\sin\theta$$

For a uniform meterstick pivoted at one end:
- Weight acts at center of mass (50 cm mark)
- Torque due to weight: $\tau = mg \cdot \frac{L}{2} \cdot \sin\theta$

### When Released
- Net torque determines angular acceleration
- $\sum \tau = I\alpha$

### Why Everyone Got It Right
- Torque from weight is straightforward when pivot at end
- Position vector r from pivot to center of mass = L/2
- Force perpendicular component = mg
- Simple calculation: $\tau = (L/2)(mg) = mgL/2$

### Key Formula
$$\tau = r_\perp F = rF_\perp$$

Either using perpendicular components or the full formula with sinθ.
