# Final Q09 - Vertically oscillating cylinders

## Original Question
Cylinders of various masses are individually hung on a spring of spring constant $k$ and set into simple harmonic motion. The angular frequency $\omega$ is determined. Then, the square of $\omega$ is plotted as a function of the inverse of the cylinder weight, as shown in the graph. Which of the following correctly determines the spring constant $k$ and provides correct justification?
(A) $k = 150 \text{ N/m}$, because the slope of the best-fit line is equal to $k$.
(B) $k = 15 \text{ N/m}$, because the slope of the best-fit line is equal to $g \times k$.
(C) $k = 6.7 \text{ N/m}$, because the slope of the best-fit line is equal to $1/k$.
(D) $k = 6.7 \times 10^{-2} \text{ N/m}$, because the slope of the best-fit line is equal to $g/k$.
(E) $k$ cannot be determined from the graph, because $\omega$ does not depend on $k$.

## Answer Key: C

**Explanation:**
The relationship between the angular frequency and the mass for a spring is $\omega = \sqrt{\frac{k}{m}}$, where $m$ is the mass of the object. Squaring angular frequency yields the relationship $\omega^2 = k\frac{1}{m} = kg\frac{1}{mg}$. This equation describes a graph in which the graphed line is a straight line with slope $= kg$ when $\omega^2$ is plotted against $\frac{1}{\text{weight}}$. Based on the given graph, the slope of the best-fit line is estimated to be $150 \text{ N/s}^2$. Thus, $k = \frac{\text{slope}}{g} \approx 15 \text{ N/m}$.
