# Final Q14 - Two Objects connected by a string over a pulley

## Original Question
Two objects of mass $M_1$ and $M_2$ are connected by a string that passes over a pulley with negligible mass and negligible friction, as shown. A group of students wants to use this setup to measure the acceleration due to gravity $g$. Which of the following procedures could the students use?
(A) Use equal masses, collect velocity data for $M_2$, and calculate its acceleration.
(B) Use equal masses, collect position data for $M_2$, and calculate its average velocity.
(C) Use unequal masses, collect velocity data for $M_2$, and calculate its acceleration.
(D) Use unequal masses, collect position data for $M_2$, and calculate its average velocity.
(E) Use different sets of equal masses, collect velocity data for each value of $M_2$, and calculate the average acceleration.

## Answer Key: D

**Explanation:**
Both objects have the same magnitude of acceleration $a$ because the objects move together as a system. If $M_1 > M_2$, we assume that $M_1$ moves downward and $M_2$ moves upward. The tension from the string $F_T$ is exerted upward on each object. We have $F_T - M_2g = M_2a$ and $F_T - M_1g = -M_1a$ which, when combined, the acceleration of the system is $a = \frac{M_1 - M_2}{M_1 + M_2}g$. As a result, if equal masses are used, then the acceleration is zero. To determine $g$, the acceleration must be determined. The acceleration can be determined by graphing velocity versus time data and determining the slope of the line.
