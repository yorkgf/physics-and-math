# Final Q25 - Sphere connected to rod moving in a circle

## Original Question
A sphere of mass $m$ is connected to a lightweight rod of length $L$ and moves at a constant speed greater than $\sqrt{Lg}$ in a vertical circle, as shown. The magnitudes of the forces the rod exerts on the sphere at positions A (top), B (right), and C (bottom) are $F_A$, $F_B$, and $F_C$, respectively. Which of the following expressions is equal to $2mg$?
(A) $F_A + F_B$
(B) $F_B + F_C$
(C) $F_C + F_A$
(D) $F_B - F_A$
(E) $F_C - F_A$

## Answer Key: B

**Explanation:**
The sphere is in uniform circular motion, so the net force at any point is directed toward the center, with a magnitude equal to $mv^2/L$, where $v$ is the speed of the sphere. At Point A, the forces exerted on the sphere are its weight ($mg$) exerted downward and the force from the rod is also exerted downward. Applying Newton's second law yields $-F_A - mg = -m\frac{v^2}{L}$. At Point C, the forces exerted on the sphere are the same, except that the force from the rod is exerted upward. Applying Newton's second law yields $F_C - mg = m\frac{v^2}{L}$. Adding these equations gives the relationship $F_C - F_A - 2mg = 0$ which can be simplified to $F_C - F_A = 2mg$.
