# Final Q02 - Angular velocity of platform after child jumps

## Original Question
A circular platform has a radius $R$ and rotational inertia $I$. The platform rotates around a fixed pivot at its center with negligible friction and an initial angular velocity $\omega$. A child of mass $m$ (represented by the small circle in the figure above) runs tangentially with speed $v$ and jumps on the outer edge of the platform. When the child is standing on the outer edge of the platform, the new angular velocity is
(A) $\omega$
(B) $\frac{I\omega}{I+mR^2}$
(C) $\frac{I\omega+mvR}{I+mR^2}$
(D) $\left(\frac{I\omega^2 - mv^2}{I}\right)^{1/2}$
(E) $\left(\frac{I\omega^2 - mv^2}{I+mR^2}\right)^{1/2}$

## Answer Key: B

### Key Concepts
- **Angular momentum conservation**: $L = I\omega = constant$
- When child jumps off rotating platform, $I$ decreases → $\omega$ increases
- $L_{initial} = L_{final}$

### Why Everyone Got It Wrong
- This requires understanding that platform SPEEDS UP when child leaves (moment of inertia decreases)
- Many thought platform would slow down or stop
