# Final Q12 - Total mechanical energy

## Original Question
The graph shown above represents the displacement as a function of time for an object with mass $3.0 \text{ kg}$ attached to an ideal spring. The spring has been stretched and released at time zero, allowing the object to oscillate about its equilibrium position. Calculate the total mechanical energy of this system.
(A) $0.0015 \text{ J}$
(B) $0.047 \text{ J}$
(C) $0.28 \text{ J}$
(D) $0.15 \text{ J}$
(E) $1,500 \text{ J}$

## Answer Key: D

**Explanation:**
From $v = A\omega$, it can be shown that $\omega = \frac{2\pi}{T} = \sqrt{\frac{k}{m}}$. So $k = \frac{4\pi^2m}{T^2}$. Substituting into $E = U_{\text{max}} = \frac{1}{2}kA^2 = \frac{2\pi^2mA^2}{T^2}$. From the graph, $T = 2.0 \text{ s}$, and $A = 10 \text{ cm} = 0.1 \text{ m}$. Substituting into the equation above yields $E = \frac{2\pi^2(3.0\text{kg})(0.1\text{m})^2}{(2.0\text{s})^2} = 0.15 \text{ J}$.
