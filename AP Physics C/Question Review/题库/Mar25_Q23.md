# Mar25_Q23 - Rolling - heights of 2 cylinders rolling up ramps

## Question Topic
滚动与能量 (Rolling Kinematics and Energy)

## Original Question
Two uniform, solid cylinders, A and B, each roll without slipping along a horizontal surface with the same initial translational velocity $v_0$ to the right, as shown in the figure. They each then roll without slipping up a ramp and rise to a maximum height $h_A$ for Cylinder A and $h_B$ for Cylinder B. Cylinder A has four times the mass and twice the radius of Cylinder B, and the rotational inertia about the center of a uniform, solid cylinder of mass $m$ and radius $r$ is $I = \frac{1}{2} m r^2$. What is the ratio $\frac{h_A}{h_B}$ of the heights reached by the cylinders?

(A) 1/1
(B) 2/1
(C) 4/1
(D) 16/1

## Answer Key
**Correct Answer: A**

**Explanation:**
Using conservation of energy: $U_{g0} + K_{\text{trans}} + K_{\text{rot}} = U_{gf}$, or $0 + \frac{1}{2} m v_{\text{cm}}^2 + \frac{1}{2} (\frac{1}{2} m r^2) \omega^2 = mg \Delta y$. Making the substitution $\omega = \frac{v_{\text{cm}}}{r}$ due to the cylinders not slipping yields $\frac{1}{2} m v_{\text{cm}}^2 + \frac{1}{4} m r^2 \frac{v_{\text{cm}}^2}{r^2} = mg \Delta y$. Canceling the terms $m$ (common factor) and $r$ results in an equation that is independent of cylinder mass and radius, therefore both disks reach the same height.
