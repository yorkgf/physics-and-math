# Q19 - Impulse - collision

## Question Topic
碰撞冲量 (Impulse in Collision)

## Original Question
Object A has a mass of m and is moving in the +x direction. It collides with object B, which has a mass of 2m and is initially at rest. After the collision, object A has a final velocity of v in the -x direction. Which of the following best describes the impulse object A exerts on object B during the collision?
(A)
(B)
(C)
(D)
(E)

## Answer Key

**Explanation:**
The impulse object A exerts on object B will be equal and opposite the impulse of B on A. The impulse on A can be calculated by its change in momentum. Therefore, the impulse on object B is the opposite of A's momentum change.
**Correct Answer: C**

## Analysis

This collision problem requires applying the impulse-momentum theorem.

### Impulse-Momentum Theorem
$$\vec{J} = \Delta \vec{p} = \vec{p}_f - \vec{p}_i$$

For a collision:
- Impulse equals change in momentum
- Direction matters (vector!)

### Collision Analysis Steps
1. Define positive direction
2. Calculate initial momentum: $\vec{p}_i = m\vec{v}_i$
3. Calculate final momentum: $\vec{p}_f = m\vec{v}_f$
4. Impulse: $\vec{J} = \vec{p}_f - \vec{p}_i$

### Why So Few Correct?
- **Answer A (50%)**: Calculated momentum incorrectly or used wrong direction
- Students may have confused impulse with momentum itself
- Sign errors common in collision problems

### Key Insight
Impulse = Final Momentum - Initial Momentum
Not just "momentum" but the **CHANGE in momentum**
