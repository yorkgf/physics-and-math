# Final Q30 - Sliding with fiction

## Original Question
A box of mass $M$ is sliding along a rough surface with initial velocity $v$. After traveling a distance $x$ it comes to rest due to the friction between the box and the ground. What is the coefficient of kinetic friction between the box and the ground?
(A) $\frac{v^2}{2gx}$
(B) $\frac{2v^2}{Mgx}$
(C) $\frac{2v^2}{gx}$
(D) $\frac{2v}{Mgx}$
(E) $\frac{v}{2gx}$

## Answer Key: A

**Explanation:**
The kinetic energy of the block is dissipated by the friction force, so the block stops moving. Since this is on a level surface, the normal force of the surface on the block is equal to the weight of the block: $F_N = Mg$. The friction force that acts on the block as it moves is equal to $\mu F_N$, which equals $\mu Mg$. $|\Delta K| = |\mu Mg \cos(180^\circ)x|$. $\frac{1}{2}Mv^2 = \mu Mgx$. $\mu = \frac{v^2}{2gx}$.
