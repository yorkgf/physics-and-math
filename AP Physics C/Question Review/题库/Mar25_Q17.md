# Mar25_Q17 - Represent and analyze - SHM ampl from a-vs-t graph

## Question Topic
简谐振动图像 (SHM Graphs)

## Original Question
A sphere-spring system is made to vertically oscillate with simple harmonic motion. For which of the following graphs of acceleration $a$ versus time $t$ does the sphere have the greatest amplitude of DISPLACEMENT?

(A) Graph with $a_{\text{max}}=5 \text{ m/s}^2$, $T=1.0 \text{ s}$
(B) Graph with $a_{\text{max}}=4 \text{ m/s}^2$, $T=2.0 \text{ s}$
(C) Graph with $a_{\text{max}}=2 \text{ m/s}^2$, $T=3.0 \text{ s}$
(D) Graph with $a_{\text{max}}=1 \text{ m/s}^2$, $T=4.0 \text{ s}$

## Answer Key
**Correct Answer: C**

**Explanation:**
From the graph it is seen that the maximum acceleration is $a_{\text{max}} = 2 \text{ m/s}^2$ and that the period is $T = 3 \text{ s}$. The displacement amplitude and maximum acceleration are related by the equation $a_{\text{max}} = A \omega^2$ or $A = \frac{a_{\text{max}}}{\omega^2}$. The angular frequency and period are inversely proportional, so $A \propto a_{\text{max}} T^2$. It is not necessary to calculate the actual amplitudes for the graphs. The quantity $a_{\text{max}} T^2$ has its greatest value of $(2 \text{ m/s}^2)(3 \text{ s})^2 = 18 \text{ m}$ for the graph shown in this answer. Note that the actual amplitude is calculated by $A = \frac{a_{\text{max}} T^2}{4 \pi^2} = \frac{18 \text{ m}}{4 \pi^2} = 0.46 \text{ m}$.
