# Q06 - Energy of two block-spring system

## Question Topic
双物体-弹簧系统能量 (Two Block-Spring System Energy)

## Original Question
In the system of two blocks and a spring shown above, blocks 1 and 2 are connected by a string that passes over a pulley. The initially unstretched spring connects block 1 to a rigid wall. Block 1 is released from rest, initially slides to the right, and is eventually brought to rest by the spring and by friction on the horizontal surface. Which of the following is true of the energy of the system during this process?
(A) The total mechanical energy of the system is conserved.
(B) The total mechanical energy of the system increases.
(C) The energy lost to friction is equal to the gain in the potential energy of the spring.
(D) The potential energy lost by block 2 is less in magnitude than the potential energy gained by the spring.
(E) The potential energy lost by block 2 is greater in magnitude than the potential energy gained by the spring.

## Answer Key
**Correct Answer: B**

## Analysis

This was the most challenging question for the class, with only 1 correct answer.

### Two Block-Spring System Analysis
Typically involves:
- Two masses connected by a spring
- Motion constrained by the spring
- Total mechanical energy conserved (if no friction)

### Total Energy in Spring-Mass System
$$E_{total} = \frac{1}{2}mv_1^2 + \frac{1}{2}mv_2^2 + \frac{1}{2}kx^2 + \frac{1}{2}ky^2$$

Or for connected masses:
$$E_{total} = \frac{1}{2}(m_1+m_2)v_{cm}^2 + \frac{1}{2}k(\Delta x)^2$$

### Why So Difficult?
- Requires correctly identifying the effective mass in SHM
- Need to account for center of mass motion separately from relative motion
- Many students chose E, possibly guessing without proper analysis
