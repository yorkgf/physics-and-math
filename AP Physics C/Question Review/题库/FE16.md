# Final Q16 - V-t graph for coffee filter lab

## Original Question
A student is investigating the motion of a coffee filter as it falls to the ground through the air. The student is using a motion detector to gather data for the motion of the coffee filter as it is dropped from rest.
Data from the motion detector is used to produce the graph shown for the speed as a function of time for the filter as it falls to the ground. Which of the following describes the speed and magnitude of acceleration of the coffee filter as it falls?
(A) Speed: Increasing, Magnitude of the Acceleration: Increasing
(B) Speed: Decreasing, Magnitude of the Acceleration: Decreasing
(C) Speed: Increasing, Magnitude of the Acceleration: Decreasing
(D) Speed: Decreasing, Magnitude of the Acceleration: Increasing
(E) Speed: Increasing, Magnitude of the Acceleration: Remains the same

## Answer Key: E

**Explanation:**
The graph shows the values of the speed increasing. The magnitude of the acceleration is the slope of the graph, which is decreasing toward a value of zero for the horizontal asymptote.
