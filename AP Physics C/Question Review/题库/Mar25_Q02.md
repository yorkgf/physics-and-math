# Mar25_Q02 - Torque and alpha - two spheres on rod - M0 and 2M0

## Question Topic
转矩与角加速度 (Torque and Angular Acceleration)

## Original Question
A thin rod of negligible mass has spheres of different masses attached to its ends, as shown in the figure. The rod is free to rotate about an axis perpendicular to the plane of the figure and located at a triangular fulcrum that supports the rod. If the rod is held in place horizontally and then released from rest, what is the magnitude of the angular acceleration of the rod-spheres system immediately after the rod is released?

(A) $\frac{1}{11} \frac{g}{D_0}$
(B) $\frac{1}{7} \frac{g}{D_0}$
(C) $\frac{1}{3} \frac{g}{D_0}$
(D) $\frac{5}{11} \frac{g}{D_0}$

## Answer Key
**Correct Answer: A**

**Explanation:**
The angular acceleration can be found using the equation $\alpha_{\text{sys}} = \frac{\Sigma \tau}{I_{\text{sys}}}$. The net torque is $\Sigma \tau = (M_0 g)(3 D_0) - (2 M_0 g)(D_0)$, which equals $M_0 g D_0$. The rotational inertia is $\Sigma m_i r_i^2 = (M_0)(3 D_0)^2 + (2 M_0)(D_0)^2$, which equals $11 M_0 {D_0}^2$. Substituting into the equation for $\alpha_{\text{sys}}$ gives $\frac{\Sigma \tau}{I_{\text{sys}}} = \frac{M_0 g D_0}{11 M_0 {D_0}^2}$, which simplifies to this answer.
