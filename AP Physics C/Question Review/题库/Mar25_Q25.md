# Mar25_Q25 - Kinetic energy and angular momentum of satellite

## Question Topic
卫星轨道模型 (Satellite Orbits)

## Original Question
The figure above represents satellites $S_2$ and $S_3$ of equal mass orbiting Earth in circles of radii $2 R_e$ and $3 R_e$, respectively, where $R_e$ is the radius of Earth. How do the kinetic energy and the angular momentum of $S_3$ compare with those of $S_2$?

(A) Kinetic Energy: Less for $S_3$, Angular Momentum: Greater for $S_3$
(B) Kinetic Energy: Greater for $S_3$, Angular Momentum: Greater for $S_3$
(C) Kinetic Energy: The Same for both, Angular Momentum: The Same for both
(D) Kinetic Energy: Less for $S_3$, Angular Momentum: Less for $S_3$
(E) Kinetic Energy: Greater for $S_3$, Angular Momentum: Less for $S_3$

## Answer Key
**Correct Answer: A**

**Explanation:**
Orbital speed is given by $v = \sqrt{\frac{GM}{r}}$. As $r$ increases ($S_3$ has larger radius), $v$ decreases. Thus, the kinetic energy $K = \frac{1}{2}mv^2$ is less for $S_3$.
Angular momentum is $L = mvr = m\sqrt{\frac{GM}{r}}r = m\sqrt{GMr}$. As $r$ increases, angular momentum increases. Thus, it is greater for $S_3$.
