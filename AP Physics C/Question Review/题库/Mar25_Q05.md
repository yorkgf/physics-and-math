# Mar25_Q05 - Torque and alpha - torque from omega-vs-time graph

## Question Topic
转矩与角加速度 (Torque and Angular Acceleration)

## Original Question
A horizontal disk is free to rotate about a vertical axis through its center. The disk is rotating with a constant angular velocity in the positive direction when, starting at time zero, a torque is exerted on the disk, resulting in the motion shown in the graph of angular velocity as a function of time. Which of the following statements describes what happens to the magnitude of the net torque on the disk for times greater than zero, and provides reasoning to support the claim?

(A) It increases. The area under the curve increases with time.
(B) It increases. The second derivative of the curve is positive.
(C) It decreases. The slope of the curve decreases as time increases.
(D) It decreases. The angular speed value decreases as time increases.

## Answer Key
**Correct Answer: C**

**Explanation:**
The slope of the graph is the angular acceleration, which is directly related to the net torque: $\alpha_{\text{sys}} = \frac{\tau_{\text{net}}}{I_{\text{sys}}}$. This means that, as the slope decreases in magnitude, the net torque must also decrease in magnitude.
