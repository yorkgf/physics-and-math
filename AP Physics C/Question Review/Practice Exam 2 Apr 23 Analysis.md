1. **FRQ Performance Gap** — FRQ average (40.7%) lags significantly behind MCQ (56.8%). 5/7 students scored below 50% on FRQ, indicating a class-wide weakness in free-response format.
2. **Problem-Solving Framework** — Students need structured approach: (a) identify given/known, (b) draw diagrams, (c) write relevant equations, (d) solve symbolically before plugging numbers, (e) check units/dimensions.
3. **Written Justifications** — FRQ requires explaining reasoning in words. Practice "justify your answer" prompts regularly in class.
4. **Multi-Step Derivations** — Many FRQ questions build on previous parts. Students must learn to check consistency across sub-questions and use intermediate results.
5. **Targeted FRQ Practice** — Jerry, lion, Lucy, Gavin, and Zihao (5/7 students) need intensive FRQ workshops focusing on equation setup and solution structure. Assign timed FRQ practice weekly.
