# QA25: Precondition for findAvg Method

## Question
findAvg computes sum/values.length for double[] array. What precondition is needed?

## Answer Key
**D** — No precondition necessary; the method will always work as intended.

方法对任何非空double数组都能正确计算平均值。题目已说明数组至少有一个元素，排除了除以0的问题。
